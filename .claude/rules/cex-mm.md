# cex-mm stack and trading invariants

## Crate boundaries

- `mm-core`: pure quote, fair-value, regime, OFI, markout and economics math. No I/O. Every behavior change comes with a unit test in the same module.
- `mm-delta`, `mm-bybit`, `mm-binance`: thin venue I/O. Parse venue JSON defensively (skip malformed messages, never panic). No secrets in logs.
- `mm-bot`: orchestration only (per-symbol loop, OMS, risk, recorder, CLI). Quote math stays in `mm-core`.
- `mm-oems`: book of record and process supervisor. It never computes quotes.
- Rust 2021, tokio, workspace dependency versions from `cex-mm/Cargo.toml`. Do not add heavy dependencies without a reason.
- Business numbers come from `STRATEGY.md` / `resource/`. Do not write parallel strategy docs inside `cex-mm/` (runbooks in `docs/` and `README.md` are fine).

## Config

- Every new env knob gets a default in code and a line in `config/example.env`. Follow the existing per-symbol override pattern (`KEY_ETHUSDT`) where sizing already uses it.
- Secrets only in gitignored `cex-mm/.env` and `cex-mm/quote-bot/.env`.

## Trading invariants (do not break without the user's explicit approval)

1. Quoting orders are post-only. The only taker path is reduce-only for a hard-cap or risk exit.
2. Cancel-all stays reachable: `mm-bot cancel-all`, `scripts/fire_drill.sh`, and cancel on start. Any new order-entry path must be covered by cancel-all.
3. On stale book, L3 regime, or reject storm, cancel before re-quoting.
4. Signed markouts are logged before any edge claim. Spread is tightened only on markout evidence.
5. Respect venue rate limits (Delta: 20,000 weight per 5 min; place/edit 5, batch 25).

## Known code gaps (verify before relying on the feature)

- `StrategyConfig::as_half_spread_bps` vol term uses σ as a fraction and is ~0; the half-spread is effectively `MM_MIN_HALF_SPREAD_BPS`.
- `KappaEstimator` in `runner.rs` is never fed; κ is always `MM_AS_KAPPA`.
- `FillEconomics::on_fill` does not realize PnL when a long closes to flat or on partial reduces; Delta fees are passed as 0.
- The daily loss stop, taker-fill guard and Delta dead man's switch run on live runs only (`mm-bot/src/account.rs`); a dry-run has no keys and none of them. A mainnet start is also refused when Delta's order leverage on a quoted product is above `MM_MAX_LEVERAGE` (default 5) or cannot be read; demo only warns. That endpoint's response shape comes from Delta's python client and ccxt and is not yet checked against a live account. Verified on demo with `mm-delta/examples/dism_safety_smoke.rs` (crossing post-only edits are rejected; a missed heartbeat cancels resting orders). Delta's swagger is wrong about the heartbeat `impact` field; follow the written "Deadman Switch" guide. By 19 Oct 2026 the create call also required `default_ttl`: without it Delta answers bad_schema ("default_ttl is required") and a start with `MM_DMS_TTL_MS` set refuses, which the PR E demo QA hit. The bot now sends its TTL there. Re-checked on demo after that change (19 Oct 2026): the switch armed at start, and after a SIGKILL Delta cancelled the resting quote 12–13 s later (TTL 15 s, acks every 5 s).
- The Binance-anchored fair (`MM_FAIR_ANCHOR_WEIGHT`) reads its basis from `MM_BASIS_SOURCE`: `book` (the Delta L1 mid, skipped while our quote is the touch, which live is about 95% of the time) or, since 2026-10-15, `depth` (the `ob_l2` depth mid our quotes cannot move; max age 30 s). While anchored, the OFI drift is off, since its denominator is the L1 size. A live `run` refuses an anchor unless `MM_FAIR_ANCHOR_LIVE=depth` names the depth source (`mm_core::fair::live_anchor_check`, both venues); `scripts/live.sh` checks the same rule before launching (until 2026-10-20 it refused any anchor weight; the gate in `plans/live-basis.plan.md` passed for ADA and `config/dism.env` now sets `MM_FAIR_ANCHOR_LIVE_ADAUSDT=depth`, owner-approved); the OEMS rejects config overrides of `MM_FAIR_ANCHOR*` and `MM_BASIS_*`. Live, the first quotes wait for the anchor, and an anchor off for longer than `MM_FAIR_ANCHOR_OFF_PULL_SEC` (10) pulls both quotes (`anchor_off_reason` in `MM_METRICS`); a basis older than its max age restarts the 60 s warm-up. The depth basis passed PR D's replay gate on ADA (18 Oct 2026); the first anchored live run started 20 Oct 2026 (100 contracts, cap 300). Before 2026-10-13 a Binance websocket read error ended the feed task for good and the bot kept the last price. On quiet nights the anchor drops for about 10 s at a time with `anchor_off_reason="binance_stale"` (16 times in 15.6 h on 20–21 Oct 2026, quotes pulled meanwhile): Binance's bookTicker sends only on a change and `MM_BINANCE_STALE_MS` is 5000 [UNVERIFIED whether these were feed gaps or an unchanged book; `artifacts/research/2026-10-21-live-stage1-2-tick-pickoff-latency.md`].
- Before 2026-10-13 a side switched off by the plan (inventory cap, fair-jump defence) kept its resting order until its price drifted past `MM_REQUOTE_TOL_BPS`, so the cap could be overshot (`OmsState::needs_update_sides` now pulls it). Since 2026-10-14 the adding quote is clipped to the room under the cap (`side_order_qty`); fills the bot has not seen yet can still overshoot it. Until 2026-10-24 a quote placed again later in the same loop (after a failed edit, a pulled crossing quote or a cancel-all) kept the size planned before a fill that landed meanwhile: on 23 Oct 2026 a bid filled during its edit and the replacement took the position to 754 against a cap of 600 (also 625 and 687 on 22 Oct). That replacement is now clipped again by the position after the fallbacks (`requote_qty`), and skipped when no room is left. On 20 Oct 2026 the 5 s venue reconcile read Delta's REST position 5 ms after a 27-contract fill, adopted the stale figure, and the cap reached -360 of 300; a read within `MM_RECONCILE_FILL_GRACE_MS` (3000) of our last fill is no longer adopted unless the difference lasts `MM_RECONCILE_MAX_WAIT_SEC` (15) (`VenueReconcile` in `mm-bot/src/risk.rs`). Since 2026-10-25 the read runs off the quoting loop (`spawn_venue_position_poller`; awaited in the loop, a timed-out read froze quoting 3.4 s on 22 Oct 2026), and a reading sent before our last fill is not adopted either, however late it answers. Until 2026-10-30 a quote the venue showed gone after a failed edit or cancel was simply forgotten: on 29 Oct 2026 the private feed was reconnecting when bid 1640249200 filled (the only one of 1,930 fills in that run the feed never delivered), the bot forgot the closed order at 400 long, bid again and reached 800 against a cap of 600 until the reconcile adopted the venue 13 s later. Contracts the feed still expected to rest beyond the venue's unfilled size now count toward the position the loop sizes with (`LiveQuotes::forget_gone`, `unseen_contracts`) until the feed delivers them late, a venue reading adopted after them (or agreeing past the grace) accounts for them, or `UNSEEN_MAX_AGE` (60 s) passes. A partial fill the feed misses on an order that still rests is caught only by the reconcile.
- Until 2026-10-19 fills never reached the OMS: a filled quote stayed tracked until its price drifted past `MM_REQUOTE_TOL_BPS` (the side sat unquoted meanwhile), and a failed cancel was ignored and the order forgotten. Fills now go to the symbol's loop by order id (`FillEvent`, `LiveQuotes::apply_fill`): a partial fill lowers the tracked remainder; when the fills reach the quote's size the loop asks `GET /v2/orders/{id}` and forgets the quote only if it is closed or cancelled. A failed cancel asks the same way, then falls back to cancel-all. On the 19 Oct 2026 demo QA (`artifacts/research/2026-10-19-pr-e-demo-qa.md`) every fill reached the loop (54 of 54) and no side ever had two quotes resting. Delta takes an edit's `size` as the order's total, filled part included (one demo observation on 19 Oct 2026; confirmed on mainnet 28 Oct 2026: a bid of 200 with 27 filled, edited to 200, kept 173 unfilled). Until 2026-10-28 the bot sent only the size it wanted resting, so a part-filled quote rested short by its filled part (11-12% of the quoted size), and an edit down to the filled part (the reducing side is clipped to the position) left nothing: Delta cancelled the order with the reason `no_position_left_for_reduce_only` (the orders were not reduce-only) and never answered, holding the loop for the 3 s timeout (all 10 unanswered edits, 26-28 Oct; none of 32,132 answered edits asked that little). An edit now sends the filled part plus the size wanted (`oms::edit_size`, fills counted per order from the feed), and the tracked remainder after an accepted edit is the size wanted. Delta's order state trails the fills feed: 13 of 45 filled quotes still read open about 150 ms after their last fill, so the loop asks again each loop and believes an open answer only after `SETTLE_PATIENCE` (5 s). On a second demo run with that change, 20 of 26 filled quotes read closed on the first ask and 4 on the next loop (about 420 ms); none needed the 5 s fallback (the other 2 were pulled by the inventory cap).
- Delta India mainnet answers an order write (place, edit) in about 0.55 s (p10 0.53 s, from the 20 Oct 2026 live logs); the demo venue in about 0.04–0.08 s, and mainnet signed GETs in about 0.02–0.08 s, so it is the venue's write path, not our client (`artifacts/research/2026-10-21-live-stage1-2-tick-pickoff-latency.md`). A mainnet cancel is about ten times faster than an edit: about 66 ms for one order (3 samples) and 17 ms for cancel-all, against an edit's p50 of 667 ms (21 Oct 2026, `artifacts/research/2026-10-21-binance-imbalance-pull-replay.md`). Until 2026-10-21 the loop awaited the bid's order call, then the ask's, so a two-sided requote held it about 1.1 s; the two sides' first calls now go out together (`FirstCall` in `mm-bot/src/oms.rs`), and fallbacks (order state, cancel-all) run only after both have answered. Every Delta REST call is bounded by `MM_REST_TIMEOUT_MS` (3000; connect 1 s), except the account monitor's equity and fills reads, which get 10 s (`MONITOR_REQUEST_TIMEOUT` in `mm-bot/src/account.rs`: at 3 s a slow fills query skipped the taker guard 6 times in ten minutes on 21 Oct 2026), and timed at debug level under the `MM_REST` target (on in live runs: `scripts/live.sh` logs `MM_REST=debug`, about 7 MB a day; the live `bot.log` grows about 300 MB a day, nearly all `MM_METRICS`, measured 21 Oct 2026); `MM_ORDER` and `MM_REJECT` lines carry `ms`, and `MM_METRICS` carries `loop_work_ms`. A place with no answer (`mm_delta::outcome_unknown`) is tracked without an id, and the next loop cancels all at once, whether or not a quote has to move (until 2026-10-25 it waited for the next requote: 9.6 s once in the 22 Oct 2026 08:21Z Delta outage). The loop is still timer-driven (`--loop-ms`, 400): a Binance tick is acted on at the next tick, not when it arrives.
- The Delta book is polled over REST every 250 ms unless `MM_DELTA_BOOK_FEED=ws` (public `ob_l1` websocket; the shadow test uses it). Funding, mark and spot index come from a background `/v2/tickers` poller (`MM_TICKER_POLL_MS`, 2 s timeout); before 2026-10-14 the loop awaited that call every iteration with no timeout. A book with no feed update for `MM_BOOK_STALE_MS` (default 2000) cancels quotes once and pauses quoting until fresh (`BookWatch` in `mm-bot/src/risk.rs`). A book that keeps arriving but stops changing counts as stale too (`FrozenBookWatch`): top prices and sizes unchanged for `MM_FROZEN_BOOK_SEC` (30) while Binance moved at least `MM_FROZEN_BOOK_MOVE_BPS` (5), or for `MM_FROZEN_BOOK_MAX_SEC` (120) whatever Binance did. Before 2026-10-18 nothing caught it: on 17 Oct Delta froze for 44 minutes with frames still arriving, `book_age_ms` stayed 0, and the basis learned the frozen book (the recorder's `ob_l2` and the mark price froze with it). Until 2026-10-31 it watched the top alone, and our own unmoved bid and ask were usually the whole touch: on 31 Oct it cancelled 8-20 times an hour on a quiet book, each time fresh again 0.4 s later, changed by our own cancel. The fresh `ob_l2` book (15 levels a side, re-sent every 500 ms) now has to be unchanged too, when its best bid and ask are the L1 top's (`depth_for_frozen_watch`: the two come on separate sockets, so a depth book that disagrees cannot vouch for a stuck `ob_l1`); otherwise, or with no fresh depth book, the top alone decides.
- Before 2026-10-14 the private fills feed (`v2/user_trades`) delivered no fills on mainnet: on 13 Oct every inventory change came from the venue reconcile. The loop subscribed without waiting for the login reply and dropped the refusal. It now waits for the login, logs refusals (redacted), and reconnects. A run with keys (demo or mainnet) exits after `MM_FILL_FEED_WAIT_SEC` (or 2 explicit refusals) unless the venue confirms the subscription, and once quoting, a feed down for longer than `MM_FILL_FEED_DOWN_PULL_SEC` switches the adding side off until it is back (`fills_feed_up` in `MM_METRICS`; `MM_FILL_FEED_REQUIRED=0` waives both). Verified 15 Oct 2026 with the mainnet key (`mm-bot/examples/private_feed_probe.rs`): the login reply is `{"status":"authenticated","status_code":200,"success":true,"type":"key-auth"}` and the subscription is confirmed at once, so the 13 Oct failure was the subscribe sent before that reply.
