# cex-mm stack and trading invariants

## Crate boundaries

- `mm-core`: pure quote, fair-value, regime, OFI, markout and economics math. No I/O. Every behavior change comes with a unit test in the same module.
- `mm-delta`, `mm-bybit`, `mm-binance`: thin venue I/O. Parse venue JSON defensively (skip malformed messages, never panic). No secrets in logs.
- `mm-bot`: orchestration only (per-symbol loop, OMS, risk, recorder, CLI). Quote math stays in `mm-core`.
- `mm-oems`: book of record and process supervisor. It never computes quotes.
- Rust 2021, tokio, workspace dependency versions from `cex-mm/Cargo.toml`. Do not add heavy dependencies without a reason.
- Business numbers come from `STRATEGY.md` / `resource/`. Do not write parallel strategy docs inside `cex-mm/` (runbooks in `docs/` and `README.md` are fine).

## Config

- Every new env knob gets a default in code and a line in `config/example.env`. Follow the existing per-symbol override pattern (`KEY_ETHUSDT`) where sizing already uses it.
- Secrets only in gitignored `cex-mm/.env` and `cex-mm/quote-bot/.env`.

## Trading invariants (do not break without the user's explicit approval)

1. Quoting orders are post-only. The only taker path is reduce-only for a hard-cap or risk exit.
2. Cancel-all stays reachable: `mm-bot cancel-all`, `scripts/fire_drill.sh`, and cancel on start. Any new order-entry path must be covered by cancel-all.
3. On stale book, L3 regime, or reject storm, cancel before re-quoting.
4. Signed markouts are logged before any edge claim. Spread is tightened only on markout evidence.
5. Respect venue rate limits (Delta: 20,000 weight per 5 min; place/edit 5, batch 25).

## Known code gaps (verify before relying on the feature)

- `StrategyConfig::as_half_spread_bps` vol term uses σ as a fraction and is ~0; the half-spread is effectively `MM_MIN_HALF_SPREAD_BPS`.
- `KappaEstimator` in `runner.rs` is never fed; κ is always `MM_AS_KAPPA`.
- `FillEconomics::on_fill` does not realize PnL when a long closes to flat or on partial reduces; Delta fees are passed as 0.
- The daily loss stop, taker-fill guard and Delta dead man's switch run on live runs only (`mm-bot/src/account.rs`); a dry-run has no keys and none of them. A mainnet start is also refused when Delta's order leverage on a quoted product is above `MM_MAX_LEVERAGE` (default 5) or cannot be read; demo only warns. That endpoint's response shape comes from Delta's python client and ccxt and is not yet checked against a live account. Verified on demo with `mm-delta/examples/dism_safety_smoke.rs` (crossing post-only edits are rejected; a missed heartbeat cancels resting orders). Delta's swagger is wrong about the heartbeat `impact` field; follow the written "Deadman Switch" guide. By 19 Oct 2026 the create call also required `default_ttl`: without it Delta answers bad_schema ("default_ttl is required") and a start with `MM_DMS_TTL_MS` set refuses, which the PR E demo QA hit. The bot now sends its TTL there. Re-checked on demo after that change (19 Oct 2026): the switch armed at start, and after a SIGKILL Delta cancelled the resting quote 12–13 s later (TTL 15 s, acks every 5 s).
- The Binance-anchored fair (`MM_FAIR_ANCHOR_WEIGHT`) reads its basis from `MM_BASIS_SOURCE`: `book` (the Delta L1 mid, skipped while our quote is the touch, which live is about 95% of the time) or, since 2026-10-15, `depth` (the `ob_l2` depth mid our quotes cannot move; max age 30 s). While anchored, the OFI drift is off, since its denominator is the L1 size. A live `run` refuses an anchor unless `MM_FAIR_ANCHOR_LIVE=depth` names the depth source (`mm_core::fair::live_anchor_check`, both venues); `scripts/live.sh` checks the same rule before launching (until 2026-10-20 it refused any anchor weight; the gate in `plans/live-basis.plan.md` passed for ADA and `config/dism.env` now sets `MM_FAIR_ANCHOR_LIVE_ADAUSDT=depth`, owner-approved); the OEMS rejects config overrides of `MM_FAIR_ANCHOR*` and `MM_BASIS_*`. Live, the first quotes wait for the anchor, and an anchor off for longer than `MM_FAIR_ANCHOR_OFF_PULL_SEC` (10) pulls both quotes (`anchor_off_reason` in `MM_METRICS`); a basis older than its max age restarts the 60 s warm-up. The depth basis passed PR D's replay gate on ADA (18 Oct 2026); the first anchored live run started 20 Oct 2026 (100 contracts, cap 300). Before 2026-10-13 a Binance websocket read error ended the feed task for good and the bot kept the last price. On quiet nights the anchor drops for about 10 s at a time with `anchor_off_reason="binance_stale"` (16 times in 15.6 h on 20–21 Oct 2026, quotes pulled meanwhile): Binance's bookTicker sends only on a change and `MM_BINANCE_STALE_MS` is 5000 [UNVERIFIED whether these were feed gaps or an unchanged book; `artifacts/research/2026-10-21-live-stage1-2-tick-pickoff-latency.md`].
- Before 2026-10-13 a side switched off by the plan (inventory cap, fair-jump defence) kept its resting order until its price drifted past `MM_REQUOTE_TOL_BPS`, so the cap could be overshot (`OmsState::needs_update_sides` now pulls it). Since 2026-10-14 the adding quote is clipped to the room under the cap (`side_order_qty`); fills the bot has not seen yet can still overshoot it. On 20 Oct 2026 the 5 s venue reconcile read Delta's REST position 5 ms after a 27-contract fill, adopted the stale figure, and the cap reached -360 of 300; a read within `MM_RECONCILE_FILL_GRACE_MS` (3000) of our last fill is no longer adopted unless the difference lasts `MM_RECONCILE_MAX_WAIT_SEC` (15) (`VenueReconcile` in `mm-bot/src/risk.rs`).
- Until 2026-10-19 fills never reached the OMS: a filled quote stayed tracked until its price drifted past `MM_REQUOTE_TOL_BPS` (the side sat unquoted meanwhile), and a failed cancel was ignored and the order forgotten. Fills now go to the symbol's loop by order id (`FillEvent`, `LiveQuotes::apply_fill`): a partial fill lowers the tracked remainder; when the fills reach the quote's size the loop asks `GET /v2/orders/{id}` and forgets the quote only if it is closed or cancelled. A failed cancel asks the same way, then falls back to cancel-all. On the 19 Oct 2026 demo QA (`artifacts/research/2026-10-19-pr-e-demo-qa.md`) every fill reached the loop (54 of 54) and no side ever had two quotes resting. An edit's `size` was the order's total (one observation), so the loop's smaller remainder is exact there. Delta's order state trails the fills feed: 13 of 45 filled quotes still read open about 150 ms after their last fill, so the loop asks again each loop and believes an open answer only after `SETTLE_PATIENCE` (5 s). On a second demo run with that change, 20 of 26 filled quotes read closed on the first ask and 4 on the next loop (about 420 ms); none needed the 5 s fallback (the other 2 were pulled by the inventory cap).
- The Delta book is polled over REST every 250 ms unless `MM_DELTA_BOOK_FEED=ws` (public `ob_l1` websocket; the shadow test uses it). Funding, mark and spot index come from a background `/v2/tickers` poller (`MM_TICKER_POLL_MS`, 2 s timeout); before 2026-10-14 the loop awaited that call every iteration with no timeout. A book with no feed update for `MM_BOOK_STALE_MS` (default 2000) cancels quotes once and pauses quoting until fresh (`BookWatch` in `mm-bot/src/risk.rs`). A book that keeps arriving but stops changing counts as stale too (`FrozenBookWatch`): top prices and sizes unchanged for `MM_FROZEN_BOOK_SEC` (30) while Binance moved at least `MM_FROZEN_BOOK_MOVE_BPS` (5), or for `MM_FROZEN_BOOK_MAX_SEC` (120) whatever Binance did. Before 2026-10-18 nothing caught it: on 17 Oct Delta froze for 44 minutes with frames still arriving, `book_age_ms` stayed 0, and the basis learned the frozen book.
- Before 2026-10-14 the private fills feed (`v2/user_trades`) delivered no fills on mainnet: on 13 Oct every inventory change came from the venue reconcile. The loop subscribed without waiting for the login reply and dropped the refusal. It now waits for the login, logs refusals (redacted), and reconnects. A run with keys (demo or mainnet) exits after `MM_FILL_FEED_WAIT_SEC` (or 2 explicit refusals) unless the venue confirms the subscription, and once quoting, a feed down for longer than `MM_FILL_FEED_DOWN_PULL_SEC` switches the adding side off until it is back (`fills_feed_up` in `MM_METRICS`; `MM_FILL_FEED_REQUIRED=0` waives both). Verified 15 Oct 2026 with the mainnet key (`mm-bot/examples/private_feed_probe.rs`): the login reply is `{"status":"authenticated","status_code":200,"success":true,"type":"key-auth"}` and the subscription is confirmed at once, so the 13 Oct failure was the subscribe sent before that reply.
