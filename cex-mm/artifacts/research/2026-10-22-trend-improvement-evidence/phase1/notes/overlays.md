## Baseline facts re-read from the repo (for overlap judgement)

## Candidates (exact rules), with the verdict "real effect or curve fit"

1. **Correlation-adjusted gross (Baltas-Kosowski CF / Carver IDM), with an ex-ante portfolio-vol variant.** sizing. Real, mechanical, multi-asset; no alpha claim. See structured output. Plausibility 3.
2. **Linear agreement count: floor each lookback's sign before averaging ({0,1/3,2/3,1}).** signal. HOP's canonical multi-horizon blend is linear in count; GHM find the equal 1/3/12 blend *below* static 12m in futures (0.59 vs 0.64) but above both in US equities (0.51 vs 0.41/0.34). Honest coin flip on Sharpe; raises beta. Plausibility 3.
3. **Trend-strength sizing: vol-normalized return capped at 2x average (Carver) or with a dead zone |z| < 1 (BK TREND).** signal. BK 2011: SMT 1.18 vs SIGN 1.00 on 12 futures (in-sample); Sepp-Lucic: linear vs binary vs TSMOM indistinguishable net (0.47/0.50/0.55). Expected benefit is turnover, not Sharpe. Plausibility 3 (dead zone), 2 (proportional).
4. **Fast-exit veto (20-day return <= 0 -> flat).** overlay. GHM: "the fix is not to trade faster"; static 1m is the worst speed (0.36; -0.14 in 2009-19); our 1/3 weight is already a 67% veto; adds a 14.6 bps round trip per flip. Plausibility 2.
5. **Faster/blended vol estimator (EWMA 20-day half-life + 30% one-year vol; optional Yang-Zhang).** sizing. Harvey et al. mechanism; BK: YZ cuts turnover ~35%. Local vol20/vol60 dispersion is modest (0.64-1.28), so a small effect. Plausibility 3.
6. **Moreira-Muir c/RV on the strategy's own returns.** sizing. The literature's winning case is the CS momentum short leg; real-time versions fail OOS (Cederburg, DeMiguel, Liu-Tang-Zhou); locally the scalar is undefined because the book is often flat (21-day RV 0.6%-57%). Do not test as written; test #1B / #5 instead. Plausibility 1.
7. **Daniel-Moskowitz bear + high-vol crash indicator.** overlay. Mechanism is the loser leg's rebound option; the long-only book has no loser leg; locally the bear state covers 27.6% of days on which the rule holds *more* gross (2023 recovery). Plausibility 1.
8. **Correlation-spike de-risking (reduce-only when 60-day rho > 0.80).** overlay. Baltas-Kosowski's post-2008 story supports it; Pollet-Wilson's positive correlation-return relation contradicts it; no crypto evidence; binds 15.8% of days. Plausibility 2.
9. **Basket-trend gate (equal-weight 9-coin index, same three lookbacks; halve when <= 0).** overlay. Cousin of the rejected BTC 200-day filter (daily: same Sharpe, worse DD; weekly +0.06); with rho 0.72 the basket is already inside each coin's signal; binds 16% of days. Plausibility 2.
10. **Post-crash cool-down (halve gross for 3 days after a < -3% strategy day).** timing. 30 events in 6.4 years; evidence of short-term reversal points the other way; the prop grafts of the same spirit did nothing. Plausibility 1.
11. **BTC-dominance / altseason gate (BTC 90-day return minus alt average > 0 -> alt weights x 0.5).** overlay. No backtest exists in any source reached; documented false signal Sept 2025; overlaps rejected cross-sectional alt momentum. Plausibility 1.

## Judgement on "real effect vs curve fit" across the angle

- The only overlays with multi-asset, mechanism-based support are the *risk-stabilizing* ones (correlation-aware vol targeting, faster vol estimation, signal dead zones). Their literature effect sizes are small (Sharpe +0.05 to +0.1, lower vol-of-vol, lower turnover), and Harvey et al.'s own point is that vol targeting's Sharpe gain is largely an implicit trend signal, which this book already has. Expect shape improvements, not return.
- The *return-timing* overlays (bear-market indicators, basket gates, dominance, cool-downs) come from cross-sectional equity momentum (where the crash is the short leg) or from folklore, and the local diagnostics show they would bind on exactly the recovery periods that make the long-only book's year (2023, 2024-H2). They are the kind of thing the 2026-10-13 report warned against: "adding a clever extra rule".
- The two signal-mapping changes (#2, #3) are cheap one-line experiments with genuine ambiguity in the evidence; they are the best Phase 2 tests after #1, because the current {0,0,1/3,1} mapping was never justified against the standard linear blend.
- Pre-registration note for Phase 2: compare at *equal realized vol* (vol targeting is linear in Sharpe) and report turnover and fees for each variant; the 9-coin hindsight universe and the 24-perp ex-ante universe both; Delta-native 2024-08.. as the OOS check.
