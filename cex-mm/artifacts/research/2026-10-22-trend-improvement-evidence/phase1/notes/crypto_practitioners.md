## 3. Candidates (exact rules). Plausibility 1–5.

### C1. Continuous, capped trend forecast in place of sign() — family: signal — plausibility 4
Rule (drop-in for `target_weights`):
```
vol_i  = 60-day realized annualized vol (unchanged), floor 0.05
z_L    = ln(P_T / P_{T-L}) / (vol_i * sqrt(L/365))          for L in (20, 60, 120)    # return in sigma units
raw    = mean_L z_L
k      = 1.25   # fixed, not fitted: under a driftless random walk E|z_L| = sqrt(2/pi) = 0.80, so k*E|raw| ≈ 1 (Carver: scalar = 10/avg|raw|)
f      = clip(k * raw, 0, 2)                                   # long-only floor at 0, cap at 2x average |forecast| (Carver cap 20 on avg 10)
sig_i  = f / 2                                                  # in [0,1]; replaces max(mean(sign), 0)
w_i    = sig_i * (TV/N) / vol_i ; gross cap and 10% band unchanged
```
Variant B (Baltas–Kosowski TREND): `t_L = mean(r)/ (sd(r)/sqrt(L))` over the last L daily returns; `s_L = t_L if |t_L| < 1 else sign(t_L)`; `sig_i = max(mean_L s_L, 0)`.
Evidence: Carver's framework (forecast scalar 10/avg|raw|, cap ±20, FDM, buffer F = 0.10) [VERIFIED in defaults.yaml and the notes README]; his claim that continuous capped forecasts beat the binary version of the same rule across 100+ futures 1970–2020 [UNVERIFIED, memory of AFTS Strategy 7 vs 5/6; no numbers surfaced]; Baltas–Kosowski: TREND signal lowers turnover and raises net Sharpe on 75 futures 1974–2013 [UNVERIFIED]. Local: the sign ensemble is already 4-level ({0,1/3,2/3,1}); band tests show turnover is not binding (fees 0.25–1.14%/yr), so any gain must come from smaller positions in weak trends (the 2022 and 2025 losses) rather than fewer trades. Expected: +0.0 to +0.1 Sharpe, unchanged DD; could be zero.
Overlap: none of the rejected items (not ML, no fitted parameter: k is theoretical, cap is Carver's). Note the rejected "single lookback" and "signal stacking" are not this.
Data: daily closes only. Implementation: ~10 lines in target_weights; whole-contract rounding at $1k will zero out small forecasts in BTC/BNB — test with and without C4.

### C2. Remove decision-time luck by tranching (averaging the signal over all weekdays / hours) — family: timing_cadence — plausibility 4
Rule (weekly book): `sig_i(Mon 00:00) = mean_{d=0..6} sig_i^{sign}(Mon − d days)` i.e. the trailing 7-day mean of the current daily sign-ensemble signal, so the Monday order list equals the netted orders of 7 equal tranches that each decide on their own weekday. Sizing, band, TV unchanged; sig now takes values in steps of 1/21.
Rule (daily book): `sig_i(00:00) = mean_{h ∈ {0,4,8,12,16,20}} sig_i^{sign}` computed on the hourly close at UTC hour h over the past 24 h (6 tranches), traded once at 00:05 UTC (or simply the mean over the last 24 hourly closes).
Evidence: local T3b weekday Sharpe 1.12–1.34 (mean 1.23; design worst), Delta 0.69 vs 0.92–1.15, T3k hour 1.12–1.32 (design lowest) — each a ~0.2 Sharpe spread carried by a single path. Hoffstein–Sibears–Faber "Rebalance Timing Luck" [UNVERIFIED, memory]: staggered tranches remove multi-%/yr dispersion between identical strategies rebalanced on different dates. Expected: Sharpe ≈ the cross-weekday mean (~1.2) with lower path variance; the honest claim is variance reduction, not a level gain; turnover rises a little (fractional signal steps → more small trades; the band absorbs most).
Overlap: "changing the decision weekday/hour for performance" — different in kind: nothing is chosen; by construction the result cannot equal the best weekday. Also not "intraday seasonality".
Data: daily closes (weekly variant), hourly closes (daily variant) — both cached locally. Implementation: compute 7 (or 6) signals and average; `trend_paper.py --weekly` order list unchanged in form.

### C3. Passive-then-cross rebalance execution (Carver's algo_original_best adapted to Delta) — family: costs_execution — plausibility 4 (mechanism), small effect
Rule: at the rebalance time, for each coin order: place a post-only limit at the best price on our side (buy at best bid, sell at best ask) for the whole size. Poll every 5 s: filled → done. Switch to taker (IOC at the far touch) for the remainder when (a) 300 s elapse, or (b) the mid has moved adversely by ≥ max(2 ticks, 3 bps) from the arrival mid, or (c) book imbalance ≥ 5:1 against us with < 3× our size resting on our side. Hard stop 600 s; if the book is stale/frozen (no update for 2 s, or unchanged 30 s while Binance moved — the repo's BookWatch/FrozenBookWatch rules) go straight to taker. Log arrival mid, fill price, fee class per order.
Numbers: Delta maker 2.12 vs taker 5.31 bps incl. GST (base 0.0002/0.0005 [VERIFIED]); half-spreads today BTC 0.03, ETH 0.10, BNB 0.14, XRP 0.75, LTC/LINK 0.81, DOGE 1.84, BCH 2.10, ADA 4.7 bps [VERIFIED]. Saving per filled side ≈ 3.19 bps fee + half-spread + the 2 bps modelled slippage not paid ≈ 5–10 bps; cost per unfilled order ≈ the adverse move before the switch (capped by rule (b) at ~3–5 bps) + taker fee. With P(fill in 300 s) ≈ 0.6–0.8 the net is ≈ +2–5 bps per side. Turnover: daily book fees 1.14%/yr ÷ 7.31 bps = 15.6x equity/yr → saving ≈ 0.3–0.7%/yr ≈ +0.02–0.04 Sharpe at 18.7% vol; weekly book 3.4x/yr → ≈ 0.1%/yr. Carver's own all-in cost (slippage 0.56% + commissions 0.29% of capital, year 11) [UNVERIFIED] is the practitioner benchmark; the "negative drift of maker orders" (arXiv 2502.18625) [UNVERIFIED] and the repo's live −3.6 bps net60 per fill are the reasons the unfilled remainder is biased toward the trending side — so this must be measured, not assumed.
Overlap: "market making" rejected — different: a one-shot order with a 5-minute passive window and a forced taker fallback, no continuous quoting, no inventory. The trend books' taker fills are not an invariant; the repo's stated preference is post-only.
Data: Delta L1/L2 book (trend_paper.py already walks the live L2 book) and the logged taker slippage distribution from the paper books for the before/after. Implementation: small state machine in trend_paper.py; needs the private fills feed and cancel-all coverage (rules file invariants 2 and 3).

### C4. Small-account minimum-position rule with risk re-allocation (Carver's "≥ N contracts" instrument rule) — family: sizing — plausibility 3
Rule: `full_i = (TV/N)/max(vol_i,0.05) * E / (cv_i * P_i)` = contracts at full signal. A coin is tradeable this period iff `full_i ≥ 2` (start; Carver uses 4). `N_eff = #tradeable`; for tradeable coins `w_i = sig_i * (TV/N_eff)/max(vol_i,0.05)`, else 0. At $1,000, TV 0.25, vol 50%: BTC full target $55 = 0.67 contracts (~$83) → out; BNB ($74) → out; ETH 2.3 contracts → in; so N_eff = 7 and the 7 weights rise by 9/7. At $5,000 all nine qualify. Variant B (per-period redistribution): when `round(n_i) = 0` for a coin with `sig_i > 0`, scale the other coins' weights by `Σ_all w / Σ_remaining w` so the intended gross is kept.
Evidence: Carver minimum-capital rule 4 × multiplier × price × σ/τ, "so you can scale without closing positions" [VERIFIED in the notes README; book claim]. Local T5: $500 account runs 0.09x gross vs 0.12x intended (75% of the exposure), rounding noise ±0.08 Sharpe; `adv_ops/checks.py`: BTC and BNB targets of $21–23 round to zero on weak-signal weeks. Expected: realized vol back to target at $500–1,000 (CAGR up ~10–25% of itself), Sharpe unchanged, fewer 1-contract BTC/BNB flips (each costs 5.31 bps + spread for no signal content).
Overlap: "BTC-only or BTC+ETH universe" rejected — this is the opposite direction and is a capital-dependent mechanical rule, not a performance selection; at $5,000 it is a no-op. Caveat: it drops the two cheapest-to-carry names (BTC funding 0.0046%/8h vs 0.01% pinned alts) at small capital — the Delta 2024-26 line must be re-run with it.
Data: contract values (/v2/products), equity. Implementation: ~8 lines in the sizing step of trend_paper.py; rule is deterministic from E.

### C5. Ex-ante liquidity-ranked universe of 12 instead of the fixed hindsight 9 — family: universe — plausibility 3
Rule: on the first Monday of each quarter rank every Delta perpetual with ≥ 180 daily closes by trailing 90-day median daily turnover (USD); exclude stablecoin/leveraged/pre-launch products and any coin whose single contract exceeds 10% of E (interplay with C4); take the top 12; an incumbent stays while ranked ≤ 20 (hysteresis); set N = 12 in TV/N. Backtest proxy: Binance USDT-M daily klines quote volume ranks (data.binance.vision, point-in-time), then Delta 2024-04+ natively.
Evidence: Man Group "In Crypto We Trend" [UNVERIFIED]: peak Sharpe at 10–15 coins; beyond that costs and liquidity dominate. Le & Ruthbah: cost effect "very substantial" [UNVERIFIED]. Local: the hindsight 9 scores 1.25/1.12; all 24 listed-by-2020-02 perps 0.86/0.82 — but that set was not liquidity-ranked and included dead coins; top-5 (post hoc) 1.27. Expected: Sharpe between 0.86 and 1.25 with the hindsight bias removed; the honest purpose is an ex-ante universe rule, not a Sharpe gain.
Overlap: "cross-sectional alt momentum" no (every coin is sized by its own time-series signal); "BTC-only" no. Risk: Delta's 10th–12th coins turn over < $1M/day with 5–10 bps spreads; at $1k, 12 coins × whole contracts pushes more names to zero (pair with C4).
Data: Delta /v2/tickers turnover history (needs a daily snapshot job going forward; historical turnover from candles volume × close), Binance quote volumes for the proxy.

### C6. Vol estimator: EWMA span 32 blended 70/30 with long-run vol (Carver "mixed vol"); OHLC Yang–Zhang as variant — family: sizing — plausibility 3
Rule: `σ²_fast,t = 365 × EWMA_{span 32}(r_t²)` (daily log returns); `σ²_slow,t = mean of σ²_fast over the trailing min(10 y, all available ≥ 365 d)`; `σ_t = sqrt(0.7 σ²_fast + 0.3 σ²_slow)`; floor 0.05 kept. Variant: Yang–Zhang estimator on the last 21–30 Delta daily OHLC candles.
Evidence: pysystemtrade defaults [VERIFIED]: mixed_vol_calc days 35, slow_vol_years 10, proportion_of_slow_vol 0.3; "EWMAs of spans 10–50 (especially 30–35) best predict future volatility" [notes README; book claim UNVERIFIED]; Baltas–Kosowski: Yang–Zhang 1-month more efficient than close-to-close, lowering turnover and raising net Sharpe [UNVERIFIED]. Local T3e: 30 d 1.16 > 60 d 1.12 > 90 d 1.08 — monotone toward faster vol, which is what an EWMA-32 delivers with less sampling noise; the 30% long-run blend stops leverage ballooning after a vol collapse (the pre-2021-05-19 state). Expected +0.02–0.05 Sharpe, slightly lower worst day; cheap and 2 parameters.
Overlap: none (only the window was tested). Data: daily closes (OHLC for the variant, available in Delta candles).

### C7. Add Carver's breakout forecast to the ensemble (fixed 50/50 with the sign ensemble) — family: signal — plausibility 2
Rule: for L in (20, 80, 160): `mid_L = (max_L + min_L)/2`; `b_L = 40 × (P − mid_L)/(max_L − min_L)` smoothed by EWMA span max(L/4, 1) (Carver's exact rule [VERIFIED breakout.py]), so b_L ∈ [−20, 20]; `f_B = max(mean_L b_L / 20, 0)`; `sig_i = 0.5 × sig_sign + 0.5 × f_B`; FDM fixed at 1.0 (Carver's FDM for 2 rules is 1.02).
Evidence: Carver: breakout rules have SR comparable to EWMAC at the same speed and are imperfectly correlated, so the combination adds a little SR [UNVERIFIED, memory]; crypto breakout repos are in-sample only (ApexTrend hyperopt Sharpe 1.63 on 9 months; resonance-breakout +293% in 380 d with 65.7% of profit in two months) and are not evidence. Expected: ≤ +0.05 Sharpe, maybe nothing; adds 3 lookbacks.
Overlap: "ML/signal stacking" — not ML, fixed equal weights, no fitting; "single lookback" no. Low plausibility because the evidence for crypto is absent and the rule adds parameters.
Data: daily closes.

### C8. Portfolio-level ex-ante vol target with a correlation adjustment (Baltas–Kosowski CF / macro-backtester) — family: sizing — plausibility 2
Rule: `w` as today; `Σ_t` = 60-day covariance of daily log returns (annualized); `σ_p = sqrt(wᵀ Σ_t w)`; `w ← w × min(TV/σ_p, 2.0)` (start cap on the multiplier), gross cap unchanged. Closed form: `w ← w × sqrt(N_act / (1 + (N_act − 1) ρ̄_t))` with ρ̄_t the average pairwise correlation among the N_act coins currently long.
Evidence: Baltas–Kosowski: correlation-adjusted TSMOM improved risk-adjusted returns when pairwise correlations rose post-2008 [UNVERIFIED]; macro-backtester [VERIFIED README]: 10% ex-ante target via 36-month covariance, gross cap 4x, net Sharpe 0.51 (no before/after). Local: realized vol is 12.4% at TV 0.25 and 18.7% at TV 0.40 — the book runs at half its nominal target because of partial signals and flat periods; portfolio targeting mostly raises leverage (equivalent to a higher TV, Sharpe-neutral) plus a time-varying component that concentrates risk on weeks when few coins are long. Expected: Sharpe ±0.05, DD up unless TV is re-set; the kill switch and DD budgets would have to be re-expressed.
Overlap: "no vol targeting" rejected — this adds a second layer, it does not remove the first; "drawdown de-leveraging" no.
Data: daily closes (covariance). Implementation: 15 lines; needs the DD budget re-derived.

## 4. Considered and dropped (not proposed)
- Funding-timing of orders (sell before 00:00 UTC settlement, buy after): ≤ 0.01%/yr, see §1.
- Funding-cost hurdle on sizing (scale down coins whose trailing funding exceeds a threshold): a variant of the rejected "funding as a signal"; with five coins pinned at the 0.01% default there is little variance to act on; not evidenced.
- Faster speeds (Man "need for speed"): local (10,30,60) → 1.08 < 1.12.
- Regime gates and bagged SMAs (TrendCore): rejected family; TrendCore's own 2022+ Sharpe 0.78–1.00 and its 64-asset check (beats B&H in 50%) confirm "shape, not return".
- AdaptiveTrend (6-hour bars, monthly adaptive universe, Sharpe 2.41 in-sample 2022–24): vendor, rules undisclosed.
- Weekend momentum effect: day-of-week seasonality, rejected.
- Delaying entries to after a funding print when funding is high: funding as signal, rejected.

## 5. Biggest operational lessons for the live books (from all sources)
1. Costs that matter are funding (1.3–2.2%/yr of equity at our gross) not fees; nothing in execution changes that; only gross and coin mix do (BTC/ETH/ADA currently cheapest to carry).
2. The two cheapest, most robust changes are mechanical: de-luck the decision time by tranching (C2) and stop rounding BTC/BNB to zero at $1k (C4). Neither claims alpha.
3. Any passive execution needs a hard taker fallback on stale/frozen books (the 17 Oct 44-minute Delta freeze) and must be judged on logged arrival-vs-fill, not on fee tables.
4. Every single-author crypto trend repo found is in-sample (hyperopt or multi-pass parameter selection); the only multi-asset, out-of-sample-tested rule mechanics come from traditional-futures practitioners (Carver, Baltas–Kosowski) and must be re-tested on the 24-perp ex-ante universe and the Delta window before adoption.
