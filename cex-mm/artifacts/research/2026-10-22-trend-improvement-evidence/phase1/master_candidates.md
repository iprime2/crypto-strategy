## 1. SIGNAL (SIG): what replaces or augments `mean(sign(...))`

### SIG-1 Baltas-Kosowski TREND trend-strength signal (t-statistic per lookback, clipped to +/-1; dead-zone reading as variant)
- **Angles:** academic_signal C1 (prior 3), practitioner_trend C9 (3), crypto_practitioners C1 variant B (parent prior 4), overlays C3 variant B dead zone (3). **Consolidated prior 3.**
- **Exact rule:** for each L in (20,60,120): r = daily log returns over the last L days; `t_L = mean(r) / (std(r)/sqrt(L))` (plain t-stat first; Newey-West lag 5 as variant; practitioner_trend's alternative numerator: OLS slope of log P on time over L days, t = slope/SE). Mapping A (BK 2020 "TREND"): `s_L = clip(t_L, -1, +1)`. Mapping B (dead zone; BK 2013 / ExpectedReturns reading "+/-1 beyond |t|>2 else 0"; overlays' ramp: 0 if z<1, (z-1) for 1<=z<2, 1 if z>=2): `s_L = 0 if |t_L| < 1 (or 2) else sign(t_L)`. `signal_i = max(mean_L s_L, 0)`. Sizing, vol, cap, cadence unchanged. Note `t = sqrt(L) x mean/std` = the L-day realised Sharpe, so the signal is graded only in weak trends.
- **Evidence:** BK Demystifying (~75 futures 1974-2013) Sharpe "insignificantly different" from SIGN (one version: SIGN slightly higher); turnover -24% (Return Stacked summary) vs "about two-thirds" (another summary) vs "more than one third with Yang-Zhang" (conflicting) [UNVERIFIED]. BK 2011 WP (12 futures, 1999-2009, 6m lookback/1m hold): SMT 28.36%/yr Sharpe 1.18 vs SIGN 15.97%/yr 1.00 [SECONDARY, in-sample]. Sepp-Lucic 2026 (84 futures 1997-2026; docs and LaTeX [VERIFIED]): linear "European" 0.47 vs binary "American" 0.50 vs TSMOM 0.55 net Sharpe, Ledoit-Wolf p 0.62-0.96 (equality not rejected); Bussgang: a sign signal keeps sqrt(2/pi) ~ 0.80 of the signal-return covariance of the linear one. [LOCAL, overlays] among full-signal coin-days z20 > 2 on 23.2%, > 3 on 8.6% of days, so caps and dead zones bind often. Mechanism: less exposure in weak trends -> less funding paid (the book's main cost, 1.9-3.4%/yr).
- **Expected:** Sharpe within noise; turnover and funding/yr lower. At $1,000 the per-coin target (~$46 at TV 0.25) is below one BTC/BNB contract, so graded exposure rounds to 0/1 contract; judge at $5,000 and unrounded.
- **Overlap with rejected:** none (not ML, not a regime filter, not a stop; ensemble kept).
- **Data / implementation:** daily closes; ~10-15 lines in target_weights. Trials: plain t, NW, dead-zone = 3.
- **Sources:** BK Demystifying (CME) https://www.cmegroup.com/education/files/demystifiing-time-series-momentum-strategies.pdf [UNVERIFIED]; BK Improving (CME) https://www.cmegroup.com/education/files/improving-time-series-momentum-strategies.pdf [UNVERIFIED]; SSRN 2140091 https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2140091 [UNVERIFIED]; Return Stacked review https://www.returnstacked.com/academic-review/demystifying-time-series-momentum-strategies-volatility-estimators-trading-rules-and-pairwise-correlations/ [UNVERIFIED]; Sepp-Lucic tsmom_system.md https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArturSepp/TrendFollowingSystems/405871df74923634f1b1eb38e53cfcfa7fd7aa60/docs/tsmom_system.md [VERIFIED]; Sepp-Lucic paper LaTeX https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArturSepp/TrendFollowingSystems/main/papers/tf_systems/paper/TrendFollowing_PaperA_SIFIN_v1.tex [VERIFIED]; ExpectedReturns vignette (TREND/SMT definitions) https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JustinMShea/ExpectedReturns/master/vignettes/Improving-time-series-momentum.Rmd [VERIFIED]; ms-tristan deep dive (BK 2011 numbers) https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ms-tristan/trading/main/research/literature/sources/volatility_targeting_trend_deep_dive.md [SECONDARY]; QuantConnect TREND implementation https://www.quantconnect.com/research/15272/improved-momentum-strategy-on-commodities-futures/ [UNVERIFIED].

### SIG-2 Vol-normalised return z-score with proportional cap (Carver continuous forecast)
- **Angles:** crypto_practitioners C1 (4), overlays C3 variant A (3; "2 for proportional"), crypto_academic C3 (3). **Consolidated prior 3.**
- **Exact rule:** `z_L = ln(P_t/P_{t-L}) / (vol60_daily x sqrt(L))` with vol60_daily = the rule's 60-day annualised vol / sqrt(365). Three parameterisations of the same mapping, all pre-registered: (a) `f = clip(1.25 x mean_L z_L, 0, 2); sig_i = f/2` (k = 1.25 fixed from E|z| = sqrt(2/pi) under a driftless walk; cap = 2x average |forecast| = Carver's 20 on 10); (b) `s_L = clip(z_L/d, 0, 1), d in {1,2,3}; sig_i = mean_L s_L` (2-sigma move = full vote at d = 2); (c) `f_L = clip(z_L/z*, 0, 2)` with z* the expanding median of |z_L| (no look-ahead), `sig_i = mean_L f_L`. Sizing unchanged.
- **Evidence:** Carver framework (scalar = 10/avg|raw|, cap 20, buffer 0.10) in pysystemtrade defaults.yaml [VERIFIED]; claim that capped continuous forecasts beat the binary version of the same rule on 100+ futures 1970-2020 [UNVERIFIED, memory of AFTS Strategy 7 vs 5/6]; AFTS reader notes: "evidence in favour of using trend strength to size positions was weak, at least for EWMAC64" [notes VERIFIED as text]. Sepp-Lucic indistinguishability (see SIG-1). Gbadebo 2026 (8 majors 2020-25, vol-normalised EMA signals): TSM 31.96%/yr, beats cross-sectional, no Sharpe [UNVERIFIED]. Kang-Ryu 2026 (BTC): fast signals overreact, dynamic speed adds nothing [UNVERIFIED]. [LOCAL] sign() toggles a third of a position when a 20-day return crosses zero by a few bps: part of the daily book's 1,773 trades/yr and 1.14%/yr fees.
- **Expected:** +0.0 to +0.1 Sharpe at best; the crypto_academic acceptance for the daily book is trades/yr down >= 25% at Sharpe within 0.03; weekly book: Sharpe or maxDD not worse.
- **Overlap with rejected:** none (deterministic transform, no fitted weights).
- **Data / implementation:** daily closes; 3-10 lines; at $1,000 pair with SZ-13 so BTC/BNB do not vanish. Trials: (a), (b) x 3 divisors, (c) = 5.
- **Sources:** pysystemtrade defaults.yaml https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robcarver17/pysystemtrade/master/sysdata/config/defaults.yaml [VERIFIED]; pysystemtrade docs/backtesting.md https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robcarver17/pysystemtrade/master/docs/backtesting.md [VERIFIED]; AFTS notes https://raw.githubusercontent.com/majkong14/advanced-futures-notes/main/README.md [VERIFIED as text]; Carver "Some more trading rules" https://qoppac.blogspot.com/2017/06/some-more-trading-rules.html [UNVERIFIED]; BK CME PDF (as SIG-1) [UNVERIFIED]; QuantConnect fast trend with trend strength https://www.quantconnect.com/research/15875/futures-fast-trend-following-with-trend-strength/ [UNVERIFIED]; Gbadebo 2026 https://www.journals.vu.lt/BATP/en/article/view/44540 [UNVERIFIED]; Kang-Ryu 2026 https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41283-026-00234-7 [UNVERIFIED].

### SIG-3 EWMAC multi-speed continuous forecast ensemble (Carver 16/64, 32/128, 64/256; optional 8/32), fixed scalars, capped, long-only floor
- **Angles:** academic_signal C2 (3), practitioner_trend C3 (3). **Consolidated prior 3.**
- **Exact rule:** v_i = 35-day EWMA std of daily price differences in price units (pysystemtrade `robust_vol_calc(price.diff(), 35)`). For n in {16, 32, 64} [+ n = 8 variant]: `raw_n = (EWMA_span n(P) - EWMA_span 4n(P)) / v_i`; `scaled_n = raw_n x s_n` with s_16 = 3.75, s_32 = 2.65, s_64 = 1.87 (chapter-15 config, [VERIFIED]) or AFTS 4.10/2.79/1.91, s_8 = 5.3; `capped_n = clip(scaled_n, -20, 20)`; `combined = clip(FDM x mean_n capped_n, -20, 20)`, FDM = 1.2 pre-registered (AFTS table 1.03-1.23 for 3 rules, ambiguous); `f_i = max(combined, 0)/10 in [0, 2]` (cap at 1.5 or 1 if per-coin concentration is unwanted); `w_i = f_i x (TV/N)/sigma_i`; gross cap as now. Binary variant: `sig_i = mean_n 1[raw_n > 0]`. Scalars fixed before testing (chapter-15 values) or estimated on 2020-21 and tested on 2022-26 only; recompute in-sample only as a sanity check (target mean |forecast| = 10), never tune.
- **Evidence:** Levine-Pedersen FAJ 2016: TSMOM and MA crossovers are equivalent linear filters, "perform similarly for all horizons" [UNVERIFIED]; Zakamulin-Giner QF 2020: MA rules have more robust forecast accuracy than sign(P_t - P_{t-n}) [UNVERIFIED]; Carver: breakout vs EWMAC 0.93 correlated at matched speed, EWMAC(16,64) historically most profitable, faster than (4,16) uneconomic, slower than (64,256) ~ long-only [UNVERIFIED / notes]; Rozario et al. BTC 2011-19 hourly, no costs: EMA crossover Sharpe 1.35 vs SMA 1.09 [UNVERIFIED]; Man "In Crypto We Trend": 50/200 MA crossover net of costs, optimum 10-15 coins [UNVERIFIED]; Lim-Zohren-Roberts (88 futures, zero cost): continuous MACD trio 1.11 < sign(12m) 1.39 [UNVERIFIED]. Carver speed check on Delta: cost per round trip 0.002-0.003 SR, limit 0.15 -> 56-77 RT/yr affordable, so n = 8 is admissible.
- **Expected:** Sharpe within ~0.05; a cheaper, smoother position path. Span 256 needs ~500 days warm-up (Binance fine; Delta alts only from 2025).
- **Overlap with rejected:** none ('single lookback' avoided; equal weights, fixed scalars, no fitting).
- **Data / implementation:** daily closes; iterative EWMA alpha = 2/(span+1); new `forecast_ewmac()` behind a flag. Trials: continuous, binary, +8/32 = 3.

### SIG-4 Donchian channel ensemble with ratcheting mid-line exit (Zarattini-Pagani-Barbon 2025 "Catching Crypto Trends")
- **Angles:** academic_signal C3 (3), crypto_academic C1 (3). **Consolidated prior 3.**
- **Exact rule:** per coin i, per lookback n in {5, 10, 20, 30, 60, 90, 150, 250, 360} days, on 00:00 UTC daily closes: Up_n(t) = max(close[t-n+1..t]), Down_n = min, Mid_n = (Up_n + Down_n)/2. State Pos_n in {0,1}. Exit checked first: if Pos_n = 1 and close_t <= Stop_n then Pos_n = 0. Entry: if Pos_n = 0 and close_t >= Up_n(t) (new n-day closing high) then Pos_n = 1, Stop_n = Mid_n(t). While long: Stop_n(t+1) = max(Stop_n(t), Mid_n(t)) (never lowered). `signal_i = (number of channels long) / (number of channels with >= n closes)` (eligible-channel normalisation). Sizing: repo form `w_i = signal_i x (TV/N)/max(vol60_i, 0.05)` (primary, for comparability) and paper form `w_i = signal_i x min(0.25/vol90_i, 2.0)` (variant). Trade immediately on any Pos_n change; apply the 10% band only to vol-driven weight changes (paper sec. 5.1). Weekly book: evaluate the state machine daily, trade Monday. Also run the 7-lookback variant without 250/360 so the Binance backtest starts in 2020 (a 360-day channel delays the first full signal to ~Feb 2021).
- **Evidence (all [UNVERIFIED]/[SECONDHAND]: the SSRN PDF was not opened; the rule is a consensus of four independent GitHub transcriptions of the Concretum/CXO summaries that agree with each other):** BTC Jan 2015-Mar 2025 net 10 bps + band: CAGR 30%, vol 17%, Sharpe 1.56, maxDD 19% (buy-and-hold DD > 80%); ETH 27%/16%/1.51/15%; BNB 17%/15%/1.06/17%. Single-lookback BTC gross Sharpe: 5d 1.66, 10d 1.55, 20d 1.60, 30d 1.61, 60d 1.30, 90d 1.20, 150d 0.99, 250d 1.13, 360d 1.28; combo 1.58. Top-20 liquid monthly rotation net 10 bps: CAGR 18%, vol 9%, Sharpe 1.57, Sortino 1.97, maxDD 11%, alpha 10.8% vs BTC, beta 0.08. Costs: 5-day model CAGR 34% -> 18% at 50 bps; the band recovers ~100 bps/yr. Printed inconsistencies in the tables (combo MAR 0.88 vs 1.15). Independent pre-registered replication (galata-research, BTC+ETH Hyperliquid perps 2023-05..2026-09, 4.5 bps, no funding) [VERIFIED file]: BTC sized ensemble Sharpe 0.97 vs buy-and-hold 0.975, maxDD -15% vs -53%; ETH 0.72 vs 0.50, maxDD -14% vs -68%; DSR 0.92, PBO 0.63 -> "not supported" on Sharpe; the drawdown halving holds. Cross-asset mechanism: stateful entry/stop designs have ~50% lower vol-normalised turnover than EWMA/TSMOM (Sepp-Lucic 125-200% vs 300-400%/yr [VERIFIED docs]); Carver: breakout family average internal correlation 0.49 vs 0.55 for EWMAC [UNVERIFIED]; Zarattini-Antonacci 48 US industries 1926-2024 Donchian long-only Sharpe 1.39 vs 0.63 [UNVERIFIED]. The repo never tested a Donchian/breakout/trailing-exit signal (grep of the evidence pack).
- **Expected:** different speed mix (adds 5-10-day and 250-360-day sleeves), lower trades/yr than daily sign; the crypto Sharpe claim is in-sample and vendor-authored; the 2023-26 re-test says shape (DD) survives, Sharpe does not. Judge on Calmar/maxDD at equal Sharpe as much as on Sharpe; compare against the vol-scaled always-long basket (T6) too since the paper's DD claim is mostly vol scaling.
- **Overlap with rejected:** cousin of 'per-position stops' (the mid-line exit is a trailing exit) but it is the signal's own trend-reversal definition (half-channel), not a % loss bolted on; must be judged as a package. Not 'single lookback' (nine). Not ML.

### SIG-5 Sepp-Lucic "European" vol-normalised linear long-short EWMA filter LS(250,20) on z = r/sigma
- **Angles:** academic_signal C4 (2). **Consolidated prior 2.**
- **Exact rule:** `z_t = ln(P_t/P_{t-1}) / sigma_{t-1}`, sigma = EWMA vol of daily log returns, span 33, lagged one day. `S_t = c x (EWMA_span20(z) - EWMA_span250(z))`, c chosen so Var[S] = 1 under iid z: `c = 1/sqrt(sum_k (w20_k - w250_k)^2)`, `w_span,k = alpha(1-alpha)^k`, alpha = 2/(span+1) (closed form, no fitting). Clip S to [-3, 3]. Long-only: `signal_i = max(S_t, 0)` (S = 1 -> full TV/N position; or min(S, 2) for a 2x cap). `w_i = signal_i x (TV/9)/max(vol60_i, 0.05)`. Variants: single-span EWMA of z with span 60 or 120 (no short leg).
- **Evidence:** Sepp-Lucic (arXiv 2607.19497; 84 futures 1959-2026, grid 1997-2026) [docs VERIFIED]: LS(250,20) with 15% portfolio vol target, 1960-2026, Sharpe 1.10 at 15.2% vol net of volume costs (1.7%/yr) gross of fees; vs SG Trend 2000-2026 net of costs and 2/20 fees: European 0.47, American 0.50, TSMOM 0.55, SG Trend 0.47 (no significant difference). Closed-form Sharpe predicts realised per-contract Sharpe with corr 0.99 / slope 0.96 (linear) vs 0.89 / 0.73 (sign). Autocorrelation channel median 0.55 at 5 days -> 0.33 at 2 years. Vol-normalised turnover 300-400%/yr (vs 125-200% for the stateful design). Valeyre (arXiv 2504.10914): a single EMA "appears optimal", indicator baskets risk cherry-picking [UNVERIFIED].
- **Expected:** Sharpe equal to the sign rule within noise, higher turnover; adopt only if funding/yr falls and Sharpe does not; otherwise a diversifier.
- **Overlap with rejected:** none.
- **Data / implementation:** daily closes; 250-day span needs ~1 year warm-up. Trials: LS(250,20), span 60, span 120 = 3.
- **Sources:** european_system.md https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArturSepp/TrendFollowingSystems/405871df74923634f1b1eb38e53cfcfa7fd7aa60/docs/european_system.md [VERIFIED]; case_study_futures_evidence.md https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArturSepp/TrendFollowingSystems/405871df74923634f1b1eb38e53cfcfa7fd7aa60/docs/case_study_futures_evidence.md [VERIFIED]; paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19497 [UNVERIFIED]; Valeyre https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10914 [UNVERIFIED].

### SIG-6 Baz et al. (2015) MACD trio with Gaussian response function
- **Angles:** academic_signal C5 (2). **Consolidated prior 2.**
- **Exact rule:** for (S, L) in {(8,24), (16,48), (32,96)}: `halflife(ts) = log(0.5)/log(1 - 1/ts)`; `macd = EWM_halflife(S)(P) - EWM_halflife(L)(P)`; `q = macd / rolling_std_63(P)`; `y = q / rolling_std_252(q)`; `phi(y) = y x exp(-y^2/4) / 0.89`. `signal_raw = mean over the three pairs of phi(y)`; long-only `signal_i = max(signal_raw, 0)` (phi peaks at |y| = sqrt(2), max ~0.96 -> signal 1 = full TV/N). Sizing unchanged.
- **Evidence:** Lim, Zohren, Roberts 2019 (88 futures 1995-2015, portfolio vol 15%, zero cost): MACD trio Sharpe 1.111 (ret 0.172, vol 0.155, MDD 0.317) vs Sgn(12m) 1.392 (0.215, 0.154, 0.264) vs long-only ~0.6 [UNVERIFIED, secondary table]; the continuous response-function signal UNDERperformed the plain sign rule (and is much faster: 8-96-day EWMAs vs 12 months). The response function shrinks exposure at extreme |y| (overextended trends): a mean-reversion guard the sign rule lacks. Formula [VERIFIED] from the Lim/Wood reference implementation.
- **Expected:** low priority after SIG-1..4; report the fraction of days in the shrinking region (|y| > sqrt(2)) on crypto, which would explain any Sharpe loss (crypto trends run further than futures).
- **Overlap with rejected:** none (the deep-learning extensions are the rejected part).
- **Data / implementation:** daily closes; 252-day std of q needs ~1 year warm-up; ~20 lines; 1 trial.
- **Sources:** classical_strategies.py https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kieranjwood/trading-momentum-transformer/master/mom_trans/classical_strategies.py [VERIFIED]; Lim et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.04912 [UNVERIFIED]; secondary table https://github.com/jjakimoto/research-issues/issues/1869 [UNVERIFIED]; Baz et al. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/314513148_Dissecting_Investment_Strategies_in_the_Cross_Section_and_Time_Series and SSRN 2695101 [UNVERIFIED].

### SIG-7 Barbell lookback ensemble: add a 250-day sleeve, optionally drop the medium one
- **Angles:** academic_signal C6 (2). **Consolidated prior 2.**
- **Exact rule:** Variant A: looks = (20, 60, 120, 250), `signal_i = max(mean sign(P_t/P_{t-L} - 1), 0)` (quarters). Variant B (barbell): (20, 60, 250). - **Evidence:** Etienne et al. 2025 (arXiv 2510.23150, Ai For Alpha, multi-asset futures, horizons 20/60/125/250/500): the 125-day layer is redundant; removing it "consistently improves Sharpe and drawdown-adjusted performance" at unchanged benchmark correlation [UNVERIFIED, vendor, in-sample Bayesian weighting]. ### SZ-6 Baltas-Kosowski correlation factor CF = sqrt(N/(1+(N-1) rho_bar)) on the vol-target weights (Carver instrument diversification multiplier)
- **Angles:** academic_sizing C6 (2), academic_signal C9 (3), practitioner_trend C7 (3), overlays C1 variant A (3); crypto_practitioners C8 lists it as the closed-form alternative to SZ-7. **Consolidated prior 3** (range 2-3).

[LOCAL] 9-coin 60-day rho_bar p5/p10/p50/p90/p95 = 0.48/0.54/0.72/0.82/0.85 -> CF 1.37/1.30/1.15/1.09/1.07; yearly median rho 0.64-0.65 in 2023-24 vs 0.73-0.77 in 2022 and 2025-26; ex-ante book vol / rho=1 budget median 0.87. ### SZ-16 Range-based (OHLC) vol estimator: Parkinson or Garman-Klass over 30 days; Yang-Zhang (reduces to k CC + (1-k) RS in a 24/7 market)

## 4. COSTS AND EXECUTION (COST): how orders are sized, buffered and executed

### COST-1 Carver forecast buffering: no-trade band of F x the average (full-on) position, in contracts, trade to the band edge
- **Angles:** practitioner_trend C2 (4), timing_cadence C4 (4). **Consolidated prior 4.** (Distinct from COST-2/3, which use a deadband around the unrounded target with an absolute minimum of 0.75-1 contract; at $1,000 Carver's band collapses because A_i < 1 contract, which is exactly what COST-2 fixes.)
- **Exact rule:** average position `A_i = ((TV/N)/max(sigma_i, 0.05)) x equity / (P_i x cv_i)` contracts (the position at full signal = 1). Buffer `B_i = F x A_i`, F = 0.10 (Carver default; timing_cadence primary 0.20, sweep {0.10, 0.20, 0.30}; practitioner_trend reports 0.05/0.15 as sensitivity). `lower_i = round(N_i - B_i)`, `upper_i = round(N_i + B_i)` where N_i is the unrounded target in contracts. If held < lower buy (lower - held); if held > upper sell (held - upper); otherwise no trade. When N_i = 0 the rounded band collapses to {0}, so closes still trade. Replaces the current '% of target notional' band (which gives a zero-width band when the target is zero and a tiny band for small targets).
- **Evidence:** pysystemtrade docs [VERIFIED]: the 'position' method (band = 10% of the current position, what the memo's band implements) "shrinks as we get to zero" and over-trades small positions; the 'forecast' method sizes the band off the average position and trading to the nearer edge "further reduces" trading; defaults `buffer_method forecast, buffer_size 0.10, buffer_trade_to_edge True`. AFTS Strategy 8/9 use F = 0.10 with the same formula; "buffering slows down trading and allows a set of forecasts that is a little quicker" [notes VERIFIED as text]. Carver blog Nov 2021: buffering reduces turnover without hurting performance provided the buffer is not too wide; 10% of average position is his default; his 2016 comment thread flags the small-account weakness [UNVERIFIED]. No published before/after Sharpe from Carver was found. [REPO] T3c: proportional band 0/5/10/20% gives Sharpe 1.12/1.12/1.12/1.11 while trades/yr fall 278/221/192/163; daily TV0.25 band 10% keeps Sharpe 1.25 while cutting orders 1,773 -> 538.
- **Expected:** no Sharpe loss; F = 0.2-0.3 should bring the daily book to ~250-350 orders/yr at Sharpe ~1.2+, with exits also buffered (a signal drop from 1 to 2/3 that moves the target by less than B is not traded). At $1,000 the contract grid already acts as a +-0.5-contract buffer and A_i is often below one contract, so round(N +- B) collapses to plain rounding; F matters from about $2,500.
- **Overlap with rejected:** none (not a stop, not a regime filter); generalises the repo's existing notional band, which stays as the comparison line.
- **Data / implementation:** none new; plan_orders takes the per-coin average position (TV/N/sigma_i from target_weights inputs), computes rounded edges, trades to the edge; qd_engine.py gets the same knob. Report orders/yr, fees/yr, Sharpe, maxDD vs the notional band at 10/20/30% and at $1,000/$2,500/$5,000. Trials: F in {0.10, 0.20, 0.30} = 3.

- **Expected:** in a long/flat book the state fires mainly in rebounds after a crash (the signal is already flat in the crash), so the effect is to halve exposure in early recoveries; whether that helps depends on how often early recoveries fail (2022) vs run (2023-24), which is exactly where the 200-day filter traded Sharpe for DD. ## 6. TIMING AND CADENCE (TIME): when the decision is sampled and how the position moves toward it

### TIME-1 Tranched weekly book: daily rebalance toward the K-day moving average of the daily target weights (K = 7)
- **Angles:** timing_cadence C1 (4), practitioner_trend C1 (4). **Consolidated prior 4.**
- **Exact rule:** every day t at the 00:00 UTC close compute `w_t = target_weights(close, t, looks = (20,60,120), vol_win = 60, target_vol = 0.25, long_only = True)` exactly as today. Smoothed target `ws_t = (1/K) x sum_{j=0..K-1} w_{t-j}`, K = 7 primary (crypto trades 7 days; K in {3, 5} as sensitivity). Contracts `n_i = round(ws_i,t x equity_t / (P_i,t x cv_i))`; apply the existing band (skip if |n_i - held_i| x P x cv < 0.10 x |ws_i,t x equity|; closes always trade) or the COST-1 buffer; trade at 00:05 UTC with taker orders. Identical to splitting capital into K equal tranches, tranche j rebalanced weekly on weekday j to the weekly rule's target, with trades netted at the account level; the aggregate return is the equal-weighted average of the K weekday-candle books.
- **Evidence:** Hoffstein, Sibears, Faber 2019 (JII 10(1); SSRN 3319045): rebalance timing luck (RTL, the std of returns across identically managed portfolios rebalanced on different dates) falls ~1/N with N tranches, mean return unchanged; overlapping portfolios optimal in the no-cost setting; RTL often > 100 bps/yr, 100-400 bps for semi-annual 100-stock portfolios; formula L = 0.5 x (T/F) x S [UNVERIFIED]. Concretum (Zarattini & Pagani, Nov 2025; US equity momentum 1991-2024): best-vs-worst schedule ~350 bps/yr CAGR, RTL ~1/N, annual turnover and average return broadly unchanged; for a ~$25K account ~2 tranches is cost-optimal [UNVERIFIED]. Newfound: immediate trend implementation beat a 20-day tranche in only 2 of 14 asset classes through March 2020; NFUSTE uses 20 daily tranches [UNVERIFIED]. Blitz, van der Grient, van Vliet 2010 (fundamental index March vs September rebalance) [UNVERIFIED]. J.P. Morgan 2015 multi-asset TSMOM: Sharpe 1.08-1.26 by rebalance day (range 0.18, ours 0.22) [UNVERIFIED]. [REPO] T3b (weekly TV0.25 band10, Binance 2020-26) by decision candle: Mon 1.26, Tue 1.18, Wed 1.34, Thu 1.31, Fri 1.23, Sat 1.16, Sun = design 1.12 (mean 1.23, sd 0.07, range 0.22; CAGR range 2.8%, maxDD -9.8% to -17.5%, 2022 -0.3% to -7.1%; 2024-26 Sharpe 0.86 design vs mean 1.05); Delta-native 2024-26 design 0.69-0.70 vs others 0.92-1.15; daily TV0.25 band 10 (the K = 1 limit) 1.25 / -14.1% / 538 trades/yr; T3k execution lag +4..+24 h changes Sharpe by at most 0.03. [SYNTHETIC] plugging the weekly book into Hoffstein's formula (T one-way 14.2x of average gross, F = 52, S 10-20%) gives L 1.4-2.7%/yr vs observed weekday CAGR sd 1.1%: same order of magnitude. Per-weekday return series were not kept, so the tranche portfolio could not be computed in Phase 1.
- **Expected (not measured):** Sharpe ~1.23-1.27 (>= the mean of the seven since the aggregate return is their average), CAGR ~15.5%, maxDD likely ~-13% (between -9.8% and -17.5%), orders/yr between 192 (weekly) and 538 (daily band 10); funding unchanged (same average exposure); fees rise from 0.25%/yr toward 0.67% only to the extent the smoothed target crosses half-contract thresholds daily. At $1,000 the 1/7 daily adjustments (~$2 per coin) round away, so orders occur only when a coin's aggregate target crosses +-0.5 contract; the benefit is concentrated at $2,500-5,000 and must be reported per capital level. Honest claim: variance reduction (luck removal), not a level gain.
- **Overlap with rejected:** 'changing the decision weekday/hour for performance' was rejected as curve fit; this does the opposite (averages all seven weekdays instead of choosing one; by construction cannot equal the best weekday). 'Daily vs weekly cadence is understood' but this is a third option: weekly-like turnover per tranche with daily-like timing diversification; the repo's cadence comparison never tested a smoothed/tranched position.
- **Data / implementation:** none new (daily closes cached); qd_engine.py --smooth-days K (about 10 lines, replaces w with its K-day mean before sizing); trend_paper.py --smooth-days / --tranches storing the last K daily weight vectors in state.json; the daily VM book already runs daily so no new infrastructure; the weekly hand book cannot do it (7 sessions a week). Trials: K = 7 primary, 3/5 sensitivity.

### TIME-2 Signal smoothing: K-day overlapping (Jegadeesh-Titman) mean of the sign-ensemble signal before flooring (K = 5 or 7)
- **Angles:** crypto_practitioners C2 weekly variant (4; K = 7, "trailing 7-day mean of the daily sign-ensemble signal"), timing_cadence C5 (3; K = 5, sweep 3/5/7). **Consolidated prior 3** (range 3-4).
- **Exact rule:** for each coin and day t: `s_i,t = (1/K) x sum_{j=0..K-1} (1/3) x sum_{L in {20,60,120}} sign(P_{t-j} / P_{t-j-L} - 1)`; `signal_i = max(s_i,t, 0)`; vol and sizing unchanged (vol is NOT smoothed, unlike TIME-1). The signal takes values in steps of 1/(3K) ({0, 1/15, ..., 1} at K = 5; 1/21 at K = 7), so a lookback that flips sign moves the weight in K daily steps instead of one jump of 1/3. Works at daily or weekly cadence; at weekly cadence it only changes the Monday target (the Monday order list then equals the netted orders of 7 equal-capital tranches each deciding on its own weekday). Keep a --sig-smooth K knob defaulting to 1 so the live books are unchanged until the test passes.
- **Evidence:** Jegadeesh & Titman 1993/2001: overlapping portfolios (hold the last K formation cohorts, 1/K turned over per period) give "no significant difference in returns" vs non-overlapping, with smoother turnover [UNVERIFIED]; Baltas-Kosowski 2012: TSMOM with the J-T overlapping method at monthly/weekly/daily frequencies, Sharpe > 1.20 [UNVERIFIED]; Baltas-Kosowski Demystifying: a continuous trend-strength signal plus a better vol estimator cuts turnover by more than one third without significant performance change [UNVERIFIED]. Carver: smoothing is unnecessary for EWMAC forecasts because they are already moving averages [notes VERIFIED], which is exactly why a sign() signal is the case where smoothing should help. [REPO] T3b weekday dispersion (as TIME-1) and T3k hour dispersion 1.12-1.32. Stock-selection warning (arXiv 2602.00196): a 21-day MA cut turnover 82% but gross Sharpe 31% on a fast signal [UNVERIFIED].
- **Expected:** turnover of the daily book down 20-40%, Sharpe within +-0.05 (unmeasured); weekly book: Sharpe near the cross-weekday mean (~1.2) with lower path variance; at $1,000 the finer grid is coarsened by whole contracts. Success (crypto_practitioners): Sharpe >= the 7-weekday mean minus 0.03 and lower dispersion of rolling 12-month Sharpe.
- **Overlap with rejected:** 'single lookback' and 'signal stacking/ML' keep the same three lookbacks and sign function; this only averages over the last K formation days (position smoothing's signal-side twin). Not a new predictor. Not 'changing the decision weekday'.
- **Data / implementation:** daily closes; target_weights: replace the single-day sign mean with its K-day average (extend the history span by K); compare with TIME-1/TIME-5 in the same sweep (smoothing the signal vs the position differ in how vol-scaling noise is treated). Trials: K in {3, 5, 7} = 3.

### TIME-3 Decision-hour tranching: average the signal over the six H-aligned daily close series (H = 00, 04, ..., 20 UTC), execute once at 00:05 UTC
- **Angles:** timing_cadence C6 (3), crypto_practitioners C2 daily variant (parent prior 4). **Consolidated prior 3** (range 3-4).
- **Exact rule:** from hourly candles build six daily close series C^H (closes at H = 00, 04, 08, 12, 16, 20 UTC). At the decision time (daily 00:00 UTC, or Monday 00:00 for the weekly book) compute for each H the signal using the most recent available H-close and its 20/60/120-day predecessors: `s_i^H = (1/3) sum_L sign(C^H_t / C^H_{t-L} - 1)`; `signal_i = max((1/6) sum_H s_i^H, 0)` (values in multiples of 1/18). Vol from the 00:00 closes as today. Size, round, band and execute at 00:05 UTC exactly as now. Variants: all 24 hourly offsets (K = 24); crypto_practitioners' simpler "mean over the last 24 hourly closes". No look-ahead: for H > 0 the latest H-close is from the previous day.
- **Evidence:** [REPO] T3k: the weekly book's Sharpe is 1.12 / 1.21 / 1.24 / 1.16 / 1.18 / 1.32 across H = 00/04/08/12/16/20 (design lowest; sd 0.06, CAGR range 3.6%: 14.1-17.7%) while a pure execution lag of 4-24 h changes Sharpe by at most 0.03 (1.09-1.15), so the hour dispersion is luck in which close the returns are sampled on, which an average over H removes (Hoffstein 1/N applied to the signal's sampling time). No external study of this exact construction was found; it is the tranching argument applied to signal sampling. Newfound "Diversifying the What, How, and When of Trend Following" [UNVERIFIED].
- **Expected:** Sharpe near the six-hour mean (1.21 weekly, daily unmeasured), a smoother signal and fewer flip-driven orders, at zero extra executions. Success: within 0.03 of the six-hour mean with no increase in orders. Whole contracts at $1,000 collapse 1/18 steps to 0/1 for BTC/BNB.
- **Overlap with rejected:** resembles 'changing the decision hour for performance' (rejected as curve fit); differs because no hour is chosen, all six are averaged. Not intraday seasonality (no hour is favoured).
- **Data / implementation:** hourly klines for the 9 coins and the 24-perp universe 2020-05..2026-09 from data.binance.vision (reachable) and Delta 1h candles 2024-08..2026-10 for the venue-native check (both cached locally per the notes); qd_engine.py already has h_aligned_closes (loop H, average, size once; ~20 lines); trend_paper.py needs a 1h candle fetch (24 bars/day/coin; 6 x 120 closes at start) and a --hour-avg flag. Trials: 6-hour and 24-hour variants = 2.
- **Sources:** [REPO] backtest_results.md T3k; Newfound What/How/When https://blog.thinknewfound.com/2018/04/diversifying-the-what-how-and-when-of-trend-following/ [UNVERIFIED]; Hoffstein-Faber-Braun (as TIME-1) [UNVERIFIED].

### TIME-4 Two tranches for the hand-executed weekly book: Monday 00:05 and Thursday 00:05 UTC, half the capital each (or trade half the gap twice a week)
- **Angles:** timing_cadence C2 (3). **Consolidated prior 3.**
- **Exact rule:** split equity into tranche A (50%) and tranche B (50%). Tranche A: on the Monday 00:00 UTC close compute target_weights (TV 0.25) and set `n_A,i = round(w_i x 0.5 x equity / (P_i cv_i))`. Tranche B: same on the Thursday 00:00 UTC close (3 days later; Friday 00:00 if a 4-day gap is preferred; the choice is spacing, not performance). Account position = n_A + n_B; band 10% applied per tranche. Alternative that avoids half-size tranches: keep one aggregate position and on each of the two days trade half the gap, `n_t = held + round(0.5 x (target_t - held))` (lambda = 1/2 partial adjustment twice a week); preferred below $2,500.
- **Evidence:** RTL ~ 1/N: sd of Sharpe across schedules should fall from 0.07 to ~0.035 and CAGR dispersion from 1.1% to ~0.55% [SYNTHETIC from Hoffstein's 1/N]. Concretum: for a ~$25K account ~2 tranches is the cost-optimal number, more tranches erode returns through frictions [UNVERIFIED]. [REPO] T3b: in-sample the design Sun-candle 1.12 + Thu-candle 1.31 pair averages ~1.21 with 2022 at about -3.6% vs -6.9%; that in-sample number is partly the luck being cured and must not be used as the expectation; the expectation is the 7-day mean 1.23 with half the dispersion. Blitz et al. 2010 remedy: blend several indexes rebalanced on different dates "without raising turnover" [UNVERIFIED].
- **Expected:** two 10-minute hand sessions a week instead of one; orders/yr ~190 (each tranche trades half the size on its own day); whole-contract rounding at $1,000 pushes ETH ($24), LTC, LINK, BCH to zero more often in half-size tranches (T5 already shows BTC/BNB round to zero at full size), hence the aggregate half-gap variant.
- **Overlap with rejected:** resembles 'changing the decision weekday for performance'; differs because no weekday is replaced; a second day at maximal spacing is added and pre-registered as 'Monday plus the day 3 (or 4) days later', so no slot is chosen on backtest results.
- **Data / implementation:** cached daily data; qd_engine.py --wd2 and a tranche fraction (the half-gap variant is a one-line change on the trade quantity); trend_paper.py --weekly needs a second due-day and a per-tranche or lambda field in state; hand execution uses --orders-only on both days. Trials: per-tranche vs half-gap, Thu vs Fri = 2-4.
- **Sources:** [REPO] T3b; Concretum substack https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/the-tranching-dilemma [UNVERIFIED]; Blitz-van der Grient-van Vliet SSRN 1574150 https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1574150 [UNVERIFIED]; Hoffstein LinkedIn (as TIME-1) [UNVERIFIED].

### TIME-5 Partial adjustment toward the daily target (EWMA position smoothing; Garleanu-Pedersen trading rate lambda = 1/5)
- **Angles:** timing_cadence C3 (3). **Consolidated prior 3.**
- **Exact rule:** daily at 00:00 UTC compute the daily target w_t (TV 0.25). Keep a smoothed book `x_t = x_{t-1} + lambda x (w_t - x_{t-1})` with lambda = 1/5 (half-life ~3.1 days); sweep lambda in {1/3, 1/5, 1/7}. Size contracts off x_t, round, apply the 10% band, trade at 00:05 UTC. Equivalent to an exponentially weighted (instead of TIME-1's equal-weighted) average of past daily targets; choose one of TIME-1/TIME-5 after the sweep.
- **Evidence:** Garleanu & Pedersen (JF 2013): under quadratic costs the optimal rule is "trade partially toward the aim", x_t = x_{t-1} + lambda(aim - x_{t-1}) [UNVERIFIED]. AQR Portfolio Rebalancing Part 1: partial rebalancing gives a bigger turnover reduction for a smaller increase in drift than less frequent rebalancing when momentum is ignored; even 25%-toward-target achieves similar long-run statistics [UNVERIFIED]. Newfound: "rebalance partially but more continuously" [UNVERIFIED]. [REPO] daily 1.25 vs weekday mean 1.23, daily turnover 9.2x equity/yr.
- **Expected:** Sharpe between the daily 1.25 and the weekday mean 1.23, turnover below the daily rule's; exact numbers unmeasured; same $1,000 rounding caveat as TIME-1 (effect mainly at $2,500+). One parameter.
- **Overlap with rejected:** none; closest to TIME-1 (same idea, different weights). Not 'drawdown de-leveraging' (no equity feedback).
- **Data / implementation:** cached daily data; one state variable per coin; qd_engine.py --lambda knob applied to the weight vector before sizing; report Sharpe, maxDD, orders/yr, fees/yr at lambda in {1/3, 1/5, 1/7} on the same table as TIME-1's K in {3, 5, 7}. Trials: 3.
- **Sources:** Garleanu-Pedersen https://nbgarleanu.github.io/DynTrad.pdf [UNVERIFIED]; AQR Portfolio Rebalancing Part 1 https://images.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/White-Papers/Portfolio-Rebalancing-Part-1-Strategic-Asset-Allocation.pdf [UNVERIFIED]; Hoffstein tweet (as TIME-1) [UNVERIFIED]; Newfound timing-luck note https://blog.thinknewfound.com/2018/01/quantifying-timing-luck/ [UNVERIFIED].

### TIME-6 Asymmetric cadence: increases at the weekly rebalance, decreases and exits daily (signal-driven)
- **Angles:** timing_cadence C8 (2). **Consolidated prior 2.**
- **Exact rule:** compute the daily target w_t every day. On Monday 00:05 UTC trade to the full target (both directions, 10% band). On other days trade only if w_i,t < held weight (signal dropped or vol rose): sell down to the daily target, never buy. Exits when the signal hits 0 are executed the day they occur.
- **Evidence:** no direct study found. Mechanism: Newfound's timing-risk note describes the loss as "signals identify no change and we remain invested; the market sells off dramatically over the next month" [UNVERIFIED]; [REPO] T3b: the 2022 result spans -0.3% to -7.1% across weekdays and the daily rule's maxDD -14.1% vs weekly -16.9%, i.e. much of the cadence cost is late exits.
- **Expected:** maxDD and 2022 loss between daily and weekly, orders ~192 + the daily sell-side trades (perhaps ~300/yr), Sharpe unmeasured; the risk is whipsaw (sell Tuesday, rebuy next Monday) which the band and whole contracts partly damp. Lowest-evidence candidate in its angle. Success: maxDD and worst week improve toward the daily book with Sharpe within 0.03 of the weekly mean and orders <= 350/yr.
- **Overlap with rejected:** resembles 'per-position stops' and 'intraday day-stop' (rejected: both legs charged, Sharpe fell); differs because there is no price or equity trigger, it is the same signal at daily cadence applied to one side only, so re-entries wait for Monday and the whipsaw cost is bounded to one round trip per week per coin.
- **Data / implementation:** cached daily data; qd_engine.py: on non-decision days apply trades only where new units < held units; ~8 lines; daily VM book only. Trials: 1.
- **Sources:** [REPO] T3b; Newfound "Quantifying Timing Luck" (as TIME-5) [UNVERIFIED].

### TIME-7 Post-crash cool-down: halve gross for three days after a strategy day below -3%
- **Angles:** overlays C10 (1; filed by that researcher under timing_cadence). **Consolidated prior 1.**
- **Exact rule:** if r_strategy,t-1 < -3% (at native TV 0.40; 30 events in 6.4 years, 4.7/yr) then gross x 0.5 for days t..t+2 (K = 3); variant K = 1 fully flat. Weekly book: skip the next Monday's adds (reduce-only) after a week below -5%.
- **Evidence:** no supporting evidence found. Contrary: Han-Kang-Ryu "losers often rebound sharply" and Dobrynskaya's crypto short-horizon reversal driven by past losers [SECONDARY/UNVERIFIED]; arXiv 2607.27070 finds no event-invariant early-warning variable across seven BTC cascades [UNVERIFIED]; [REPO] the prop-leg 're-entry room' and 'chop detector (3 trips/10 d -> k/2 for 30 d)' grafts had no measurable effect (backtest_results.md section 5.2).
- **Expected:** thirty events; any backtest result is noise and the a priori sign is wrong (post-crash bounces). Not worth a Phase 2 slot.
- **Overlap with rejected:** cousin of the rejected intraday day stop and the chop detector; differs in acting after the close.
- **Sources:** [REPO] backtest_results.md section 5.2 and local_evidence.md; searching-for-edge literature reference https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Silent47boryara/searching-for-edge/main/07-bos/reference-3-price-structure-momentum-literature.md [SECONDARY]; arXiv 2607.27070 via RePEc https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2607.27070.html [UNVERIFIED].
