## 1. Summary

- Downloaded **324 monthly CSVs** (13 pairs, each pair's first listed month through 2020-04), **324/324** zip sha256 equal to Binance's `.CHECKSUM`. No month between a pair's first month and 2020-04 is missing from the S3 listing. Download 2026-10-22T17:49:26Z to 2026-10-22T17:50:44Z.
- Format: 12 Binance columns, **no header row in any of the 324 files**, open_time and close_time in **milliseconds in all 324 files** (0 µs, 0 mixed). 0 duplicate open times within or across files, 0 NaN, 0 non-positive prices, 0 OHLC inconsistencies.
- **Off the 5 m grid (found and repaired):** 1,205 rows (241 in each of BTC, ETH, BNB, LTC, NEO), all from Binance's restart after the 2018-02-08 outage: 2018-02-09 09:58:14.789 .. 2018-02-10 05:58:14.789, open times at hh:m3:14.789 / hh:m8:14.789. Binance's daily files for 2018-02-09 and 2018-02-10 hold the same trades on the 5 m grid (totals of volume, quote volume, trade count, taker volumes, high, low, first open and last close identical over the two days); those days were replaced by the daily rows. After the patch: **0 off-grid rows**.
- **Gaps:** every gap in the data is an exchange-wide Binance spot outage or maintenance (the same hole in every pair listed at the time). 213 coin-days touched by a gap were checked against the daily files (all present, all checksums OK): 209 have the same hole; 4 daily files held exactly one extra bar each (BTC and ETH 2017-09-06 16:00, LTC and NEO 2018-02-11 04:00), which were inserted. No gap could otherwise be filled. 20 distinct outage windows longer than 15 min remain, 15 of them 2 h or longer (section 6).
- **Zero-volume bars** (flat, 0 trades) are real no-trade bars: 27,542 in total, 14,834 of them DOGE and 4,384 LINK (thin books). Two runs are outage artefacts: ETC printed 120 flat bars through the 2018-06-26 02:00-12:00 maintenance that is a gap in the other ten pairs, and all twelve pairs listed on 2019-06-07 printed flat bars from 20:00/20:05 up to the 21:15-22:15 gap (section 7).
- **The pre-registered 99 % rule removes every long-maintenance week**: a week may miss at most 20 bars (100 min). 15 calendar weeks fall below it (in every pair trading that week; bars 1605-1995), including the **last planned event week 2020-04-20** (1986/2,016 bars in all 13 pairs: 150-min maintenance on 2020-04-25 02:00-04:30). So the last usable event week is **2020-04-13** for all 13 pairs.
- Usable event coin-weeks (week and prior week both >= 99 %, through 2020-04-20): **1060** in total, 397 in the first half (2017-08..2018-12) and 663 in the second (2019-01..2020-04). Per-coin table in section 2.
- Price prints to know about (real Binance prints, not altered): ETH 2017-08-22 04:00 (open and low 144.21 inside a ~300 market) and LINK 2020-03-12 10:45 (low 0.0001) each set the next week's PWL far out of reach; other single-bar wicks of 15-60 % are listed in section 8.

## 5. Patches (all logged in `patch_log.json`; originals kept in `replaced_monthly/`)

- Patch rule for gap bars: inserted verbatim only if every monthly row of that day has a daily row with the same open time and identical OHLCV, quote volume, trade count and taker volumes. ## 10. Notes for the Phase 2 engine

- Read every file without a header (none has one), open_time in ms, 12 columns; after the patches every open time is on the 5 m grid, strictly increasing, no duplicates.
- Count bars per Monday week by open time; `post/week_coverage.csv` holds bars, `ge99` and `event_eligible` per coin-week, which the engine's own counts should reproduce exactly.
- The plan's window ends at the event week 2020-04-20, which fails the 99 % rule in every pair; the last event week is 2020-04-13, whose 24 h exits end by 2020-04-20 23:55, inside the downloaded data.
- P3 (Thursday 12:00 anchors) needs its own coverage count over its 168 h windows; it is not in `week_coverage.csv`. The outage windows in section 6 apply to it the same way.
- Zero-volume flat bars are kept and counted. Exits that land in an outage gap have no bar ending at the exit time; the Phase 1 engine drops such outcomes (`missing_bar`), which should be counted and reported.
