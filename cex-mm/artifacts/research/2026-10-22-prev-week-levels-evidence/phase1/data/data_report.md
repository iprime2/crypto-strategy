# Panel A data audit: Binance USDT-M perp 5 m klines, nine coins, 2020-01..2026-09

Generated by `research3/data/make_report.py` from `audit_files.csv`, `gaps_all.csv`, `week_coverage.csv`, `zero_volume_runs.csv`, `download_log.json`, `patch_log.json`, `verify_existing.json` (same folder). Source: public data.binance.vision only (`data/futures/um/{monthly,daily}/klines/<SYM>USDT/5m/`). Files live in `universe/klines5m/binance-<SYM>USDT-5m-YYYY-MM.csv`. All times UTC.

## 1. Summary

- Downloaded (2020-01..2023-12): **425 monthly CSVs** written, 425/425 zip sha256 checksums verified against Binance's `.CHECKSUM` files (every month in the S3 listing had one). Download run 2026-10-22T15:54:11Z to 2026-10-22T15:55:50Z.
- Not available: **7 coin-months**, all before the coin's USDT-M listing; neither a monthly nor any daily file exists for them in the S3 listing: BNBUSDT 2020-01, DOGEUSDT 2020-01, DOGEUSDT 2020-02, DOGEUSDT 2020-03, DOGEUSDT 2020-04, DOGEUSDT 2020-05, DOGEUSDT 2020-06.
- Patched from daily files: **4 coin-months** whose monthly zip (checksum OK) was incomplete: XRPUSDT 2022-02, XRPUSDT 2022-04, LTCUSDT 2022-02, LTCUSDT 2022-04. Rebuilt from 116 daily zips, all checksums OK; details in section 3.
- Pre-existing 2024-01..2026-09 files: **297/297 byte-identical** to the CSV inside Binance's checksum-verified monthly zip. They were read only, never written.
- After the patch, all 722 files on disk: 0 duplicate open times (within or across files), 0 gaps of any length after each coin's first bar, every month complete from the listing bar onward, all timestamps in **milliseconds**.
- Every Monday-00:00-UTC week that lies fully inside the data window and after the listing week has 2016/2016 bars. The only weeks below 99 % are listing weeks, pre-listing weeks and the two window-truncated edge weeks (section 7).

## 6. Gaps longer than 15 minutes

Gap = two consecutive open times more than 5 min apart; length = missing minutes. After the patch in section 3: **0 gaps of any length** remain in 2020-01..2026-09 for the nine coins (`gaps_all.csv` is empty but for its header). Before the patch there were exactly the four gaps in the section 3 table.

## 8. Notes for the two implementations

- Detect the header per file; LTCUSDT and XRPUSDT 2022-03 have no header although their 2022-01/02/04 neighbours do.
- All open_time values are ms multiples of 300000; close_time = open_time + 299999 in every row.
- XRPUSDT and LTCUSDT 2022-02 and 2022-04 on disk are the daily-file rebuilds (section 3); a fresh monthly download of those four months would reintroduce the holes (2022-02-26..28, 3 days; 2022-04-01..02, 2 days).
- Venue-wide flat zero-volume halts exist (section 6); the longest is BTCUSDT 2023-11-10 15:10..16:40 (19 bars, BTC only).
- Files for 2024-01..2026-09 were not modified (mtimes unchanged, contents identical to Binance's zips).
