## KQ1. Trend following / CTA-style on crypto (Donchian, breakout, dual momentum, ensembles, vol targeting, regime filters): reported 2022–2026 Sharpe and drawdown, and what went wrong in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
The best-documented public trend result is Zarattini/Pagani/Barbon's Donchian-ensemble with 25% vol targeting on the top-20 liquid coins (Sharpe ≈1.5–1.6, ~18% net CAGR, ~11% max DD, 2015–Mar 2025, backtest). Live evidence is far worse: the Barclay Cryptocurrency Traders Index lost 12.3% in 2025 after +54% in 2024, and Bloomberg, Man Group and Pantera all describe 2025 as a policy-headline whipsaw year that punished trend; independent re-tests find most of the drawdown improvement comes from vol scaling, not the trend signal.

### Cited Findings
**Backtests with the most rigor**
- "Catching Crypto Trends" (Zarattini, Pagani, Barbon; SSRN 5209907 / Concretum): signal is an ensemble of Donchian channels across nine lookback horizons, with volatility-based sizing targeting 25% annualized vol; rotational portfolio of the top 20 most liquid coins from a survivorship-bias-free dataset of all coins traded since 2015; abstract reports Sharpe above 1.5 and 10.8% annualized alpha vs Bitcoin, net of fees — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5209907); [Concretum page](https://concretumgroup.com/catching-crypto-trends-a-tactical-approach-for-bitcoin-and-altcoins/); [Concretum Substack](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/catching-crypto-trends). Credibility MED (working paper; cost treatment present).
- Secondary summaries of the same paper give Sharpe 1.56 for the BTC-only ensemble, 1.57 for the diversified program and 1.58 for the "Combo" model; a top-20 portfolio net-of-fee CAGR of 18% with max drawdown 11%; sample Jan 2015–Mar 2025; fee sensitivity 0.10%–0.50% per trade [UNVERIFIED: secondary — the fee range came from a GitHub issue summarizing the paper, and one issue notes the abstract itself does not state cost assumptions] — [GitHub issue #137](https://github.com/zebadee2kk/DeFi-TraderStack-Agent/issues/137); [QuantifiedStrategies write-up](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/crypto-trend-trading-strategy-for-bitcoin/); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391508704_Catching_Crypto_Trends_A_Tactical_Approach_for_Bitcoin_and_Altcoins).
- "AdaptiveTrend" (Bui & Nguyen, Talyxion Research, arXiv 2602.11708, 2026 preprint): 150+ crypto pairs, 6-hour signals, volatility-calibrated trailing stop, 70/30 long-short split; out-of-sample reported as Sharpe 2.41, max DD −12.7%, Calmar 3.18, 40.5% annualized; Sharpe stays above 2.0 at 2x base transaction costs; the 70/30 variant beat a dollar-neutral variant by 29 bps of Sharpe. Search summaries disagree on the OOS window (one says 2022–2024, another Jan 2022–Oct 2025). Authors are a small firm; preprint, backtest only — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11708v1); [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11708v1); promo repeat at [Quantitativo Substack note](https://substack.com/@quantitativo/note/c-216039933). Credibility LOW-MED (not peer reviewed; headline Sharpe >2 on crypto should be treated skeptically until replicated).
- Monash Centre for Financial Studies project: trend-following with shorter lookbacks generates high Sharpe, "however, transaction costs may decrease profits due to frequent portfolio rebalancing" — [Monash](https://www.monash.edu/business/mcfs/our-research/all-projects/investment-strategy/trend-following-strategies-for-crypto-investors). Credibility MED.
- Han, Kang & Ryu (realistic-assumptions study): "Evidence of time-series momentum is strong, whereas evidence of cross-sectional momentum is weak"; once transaction costs and daily price fluctuations are accounted for "many momentum portfolios are liquidated and many with statistically significant returns earn insignificant profits" — [AUT working paper PDF](https://acfr.aut.ac.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/918729/Time_Series_and_Cross_Sectional_Momentum_in_the_Cryptocurrency_Market_with_IA.pdf). Credibility MED.

BATP journal PDF](https://www.journals.vu.lt/BATP/lt/article/download/44540/42590/138419) [UNVERIFIED: snippet only, costs not visible]. Credibility LOW-MED.

### Inferences
- The gap between the best backtests (Sharpe 1.5–2.4) and the one investable live index (−12% in 2025, +54% in 2024, "mirrored bitcoin") says that most public crypto "trend" programs behave like levered long-BTC beta with a vol brake; Summitward's decomposition (0.74 → 0.79 Sharpe from the trend signal, rest from vol scaling) is the cleanest evidence of this.
- The 2025 failure mode is regime, not model: fast policy-headline reversals (tariffs, Oct 10 cascade) produce whipsaw losses in any breakout system; practitioners who survived (Pedma, XBTO) did so by position sizing/vol targeting and by being able to go short, not by a better entry signal.
- For a small account on perps, Donchian/breakout ensembles with 20–25% vol targeting and daily-to-6h decisions are the only trend family with both a credible net-of-fee backtest and open implementations; expect realized Sharpe below 1 after funding and slippage, and multi-month drawdowns of 15–30%.

### Gaps
- No practitioner-published, verifiable live crypto trend track record with monthly returns for 2022–2026 was found (Robot Wealth and Pedma numbers are paywalled or self-reported; XBTO figure is one month).
- Could not open the Zarattini paper to confirm the fee assumption or 2024–2025 sub-period performance; could not open the AdaptiveTrend preprint to resolve the OOS window conflict.
- No source quantified the funding-rate drag on long-biased trend positions in perps during 2024–2025 (search budget exhausted).
- No crypto-specific BTC-above/below-200-day regime filter evidence beyond the single-year Adziima study.

## KQ2. Mean reversion: intraday/multi-hour reversal on BTC/ETH and alts, Bollinger/z-score, liquidation-cascade fade, overnight/weekend effects — results after fees

### Takeaway
The only mean-reversion-adjacent result with a named, repeatable rule is Quantpedia's BTC intraday seasonality (hold 21:00–23:00 UTC: ~40% annualized, Calmar 1.79, −22.7% max DD on 2015–2022 Gemini data, with a rough 2022–2023 patch); day-of-week studies through 2023 find no exploitable BTC return anomaly, and no published after-fee backtest of a liquidation-cascade fade or single-asset intraday z-score reversal on BTC/ETH was found.

### Cited Findings
- Quantpedia (Gemini hourly data 2015–2022): the most sizeable and significant BTC returns are at the 22:00 and 23:00 UTC hours; a two-hour hold strategy reported 33% annualized with lower vol and drawdown than buy-and-hold — [Quantpedia: Are There Seasonal Intraday or Overnight Anomalies in Bitcoin?](https://quantpedia.com/are-there-seasonal-intraday-or-overnight-anomalies-in-bitcoin/). Credibility MED (vendor research, rule explicit, backtest).
- Quantpedia follow-up: buying 21:00 and selling 23:00 UTC gives "annualized rate return of 40.64%", Calmar 1.79, max DD −22.7% versus over −70% for BTC; the strategy "experienced a drawdown" in 2022–2023; a high-volatility filter version returns 37.26% annualized with Calmar 1.96 — [Quantpedia: How To Profitably Trade Bitcoin's Overnight Sessions?](https://quantpedia.com/how-to-profitably-trade-bitcoins-overnight-sessions/); [Quantpedia: The Seasonality of Bitcoin](https://quantpedia.com/the-seasonality-of-bitcoin/). QuantConnect re-implementations use different exchange data and results vary between runs — [QuantConnect embedded backtest](https://www.quantconnect.com/terminal/quantpediaBacktestResult/embedded_backtest_fd6c44cde23da89ba73dbb7d79dc157a.html). Credibility MED; fee treatment not visible [UNVERIFIED].
- Day-of-week: Sahu (2024, The Anáhuac Journal, Jul 2020–Dec 2023) — Bitcoin "exhibits no discernible calendar anomalies", Ethereum shows a day-of-week effect — [scielo PDF](https://scielo.org.mx/pdf/taj/v24n1/2683-2690-taj-24-01-12.pdf). Yaya (Oct 2024, Journal of Statistical Research): "non-significance of day-of-the-week effects in returns", possible Monday/Friday effects in BTC volatility only — [journals.ui.edu.ng](https://journals.ui.edu.ng/index.php/jsr/article/view/636). Credibility MED (peer-reviewed but small journals).
- A Medium "day-of-week momentum" strategy claiming 308% annual return and Sharpe 2.96 turned out to be a bug — [Medium/Coinmonks](https://medium.com/coinmonks/day-of-week-momentum-strategy-for-crypto-with-308-annual-returns-and-sharpe-2-96-but-4bd4ef0a31a8). Credibility: useful as a cautionary tale.
- Robot Wealth "Quantifying and Combining Crypto Alphas": tested breakout, carry and 10-day momentum features; found a roughly negative relationship for short-term cross-sectional 10-day momentum, i.e. a slight mean-reversion effect — [Robot Wealth](https://robotwealth.com/quantifying-and-combining-crypto-alphas/). Credibility MED.
- Erasmus thesis on statistical arbitrage on the seven largest coins: the contrarian (reversal) effect dominated at high frequency — [thesis.eur.nl](https://thesis.eur.nl/pub/59629/Final_Thesis_Draft_494850-1-.pdf) [UNVERIFIED]. Credibility LOW-MED.
- Hophop Reversion Strategy (TradingView, BTC/ETH 15-min, 0.02% commission + 30 ticks slippage): author warns "on a low timeframe, commission and slippage may cost more than strategy gains" and that mean reversion on a high timeframe "could be pretty dangerous because crypto markets trend"; no performance figures shown — [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/script/khGKr44X-Hophop-Reversion-Strategy). Credibility LOW.
- IEEE Access paper on distance/cointegration pairs across 26 liquid Binance coins: 5-minute version 11.61% monthly vs daily distance method −0.07% monthly; "results are quite sensitive to parameter settings and external factors such as transaction costs"; net-of-fee status unclear — [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9200323) [UNVERIFIED]. Credibility MED.
- Liquidation-cascade fade: no published backtest found. Tools exist (TradingView "Liquidation Cascade Detector" for reversal entries, no results — [TradingView](https://vn.tradingview.com/script/9A7zNDKe-Liquidation-Cascade-Detector-Basic/); Senpi "PIRANHA"/"SHARK" skills take the opposite, momentum side, no performance data — [skills.sh PIRANHA](https://www.skills.sh/senpi-ai/senpi-skills/piranha-strategy)). A Substack essay "Trading the liquidation cascade" exists — [project10x Substack](https://project10x.substack.com/p/trading-the-liquidation-cascade) [UNVERIFIED]. Credibility LOW.
- Structural evidence relevant to fading: a preprint on Hyperliquid's Oct 2025 crash found most forced selling in the worst minute went to the venue backstop (HLP) rather than the public order book; a separate arXiv paper on seven crypto-perp cascades (2022–2025) estimates the Oct 2025 event was "deeply subcritical" — [IDEAS/arXiv 2608.03616](https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2608.03616.html) [UNVERIFIED]. The November 2025 cascade liquidated ~396,000 traders and perp OI fell 35% from the October peak — [Coinchange](https://www.coinchange.io/blog/bitcoins-2-billion-reckoning-how-novembers-liquidations-cascade-exposed-cryptos-structural-fragilities). Credibility MED.
- A Dec 2025 microstructure paper found a simple passive market-making strategy was "repeatedly filled on its bid-side quotes" during the 10 Oct 2025 crash, accumulating "a growing, and increasingly unprofitable, long position" — the same mechanism that hurts naive dip-fading — [arXiv 2602.00776](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00776v1). Credibility MED.

### Inferences
- The one mean-reversion-like edge with repeated public documentation (BTC 21:00–23:00 UTC seasonality) is a calendar/flow effect, not a z-score reversal, and it already showed a 2022–2023 drawdown; it is cheap to run (one round trip per day) but is a single-asset, long-only bet.
- Short-horizon reversal on alts likely exists gross (RW's negative 10-day momentum, the Erasmus thesis) but every source that mentions fees warns it is eaten at retail taker rates; it is only plausible with maker fills, which reintroduces adverse selection.
- Fading liquidation cascades is undocumented as a backtested strategy; the Oct 2025 evidence (backstop absorbs flow, stale bids get run over) argues that a small account cannot be the counterparty at the bottom without pre-positioned limit orders that will also fill on the way down.

### Gaps
- No after-fee, 2024–2026 backtest of intraday Bollinger/z-score reversal on BTC/ETH or alts was found.
- No weekend-return anomaly result for 2024–2026 (studies end 2023).
- The Quantpedia overnight-session article's conclusions were not visible in snippets.

## KQ3. Cross-sectional alt strategies: long/short alt momentum, relative strength vs BTC, beta-neutral alt baskets — results and the cost problem

### Takeaway
Academic evidence that includes costs is consistent: cross-sectional crypto momentum is real gross but fragile net, with the edge concentrated in large, liquid coins and, per Fieberg et al. (2024), extracted largely from the short leg, which is the expensive leg; 2025 compounded this with an altcoin bear market since late 2024 and a 60% collapse in alt volumes after 10 Oct 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Fieberg, Liedtke & Zaremba (2024), "Cryptocurrency anomalies and economic constraints": momentum prevails in larger cryptocurrencies but "incurs substantial trading costs and extracts alphas largely from short positions"; authors recommend tradable protocols "focus on long positions, account for transaction costs, consider hard-to-trade coins, and emphasize performance in recent years" — [ICM repository](https://open.icm.edu.pl/handle/123456789/25544). Credibility HIGH (peer-reviewed).
- Han, Kang & Ryu: cross-sectional momentum weak and concentrated among large winners; many portfolios liquidated once costs and daily swings are modeled — [AUT PDF](https://acfr.aut.ac.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/918729/Time_Series_and_Cross_Sectional_Momentum_in_the_Cryptocurrency_Market_with_IA.pdf). Credibility MED.
- "Momentum and liquidity in cryptocurrencies" (arXiv 1904.00890): momentum statistically significant in the most liquid coins; the illiquid-losers leg is hit hardest by bid-ask costs — [ar5iv](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1904.00890). Credibility MED (older).
- Transfer-ranking cross-sectional momentum paper (arXiv 2208.09968) assumes 26 bps per-trade cost for crypto and reports positive cost-adjusted Sharpe up to 29 bps for its best model — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.09968). Credibility MED.
- 2020–Oct 2025 study: cross-sectional momentum max drawdown 55.0% vs time-series momentum 31.96% annual return — [BATP PDF](https://www.journals.vu.lt/BATP/lt/article/download/44540/42590/138419) [UNVERIFIED]. Credibility LOW-MED.
- Cross-sectional carry (University of Manitoba study): long high-funding/short low-funding coins yields 43.4% annualized with Sharpe 0.74 — reported via a BATP journal snippet [UNVERIFIED: secondary] — [BATP PDF](https://www.journals.vu.lt/BATP/lt/article/download/44540/42590/138419). Credibility LOW-MED.
- Liu & Tsyvinski (2019) zero-investment momentum ~2.5–4% weekly excess returns (gross), per thesis summary — [thesis.eur.nl](https://thesis.eur.nl/pub/45268/Kroft-L.C.-van-der-432770-.pdf) [UNVERIFIED: secondary]. Dated.
- Robot Wealth's cross-sectional 10-day momentum feature showed slight mean reversion, not momentum, in their crypto alpha tests — [Robot Wealth](https://robotwealth.com/quantifying-and-combining-crypto-alphas/). Credibility MED.
- Market backdrop: Pantera says altcoins have been in a bear market since late 2024 — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/01/23/altcoins-have-been-in-a-bear-market-since-late-2024-pantera-says). Kaiko's Thomas Probst: average 1% market depth across ETH, XRP, SOL, SUI, LINK fell from about $2.6M before the crash to roughly $1.7M, and combined altcoin trading volume fell 60% since 10 Oct 2025 — [Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/crypto/altcoin-trading-activity-has-plummeted-60-percent-since-october-liquidation-event/); [Kaiko](https://cfc-stmoritz.com/industry-insights/did-the-october-10-crash-permanently-reshape-crypto-liquidity). Credibility HIGH (Kaiko data).
- Real-execution benchmark: 1Token's Long Short index (built from real trading teams) gained 5.76% in July 2026 after a 7.78% decline in June 2026; its Directional index fell 3.74% in July after +2.54% in June — [1Token June/July 2026](https://blog.1token.tech/crypto-quant-strategy-index-xiv-june-july-2026/). Credibility MED (vendor index from real data, small sample of teams).

### Inferences
- The "cost problem" is structural: the alpha is in shorting illiquid losers, which carries the widest spreads, the highest funding (shorts often pay when alts are crowded short in a bear) and the worst slippage on a cascade day; a long-only or long-tilted (e.g. 70/30 as in AdaptiveTrend) implementation on the top-20 by liquidity is the only version the cost-aware literature supports.
- A "beta-neutral alt basket" (short alts vs long BTC) would have been directionally right since late 2024 per Pantera, but no practitioner track record for it was found; the 1Token L/S index swinging −7.8%/+5.8% month-to-month in mid-2026 shows even professional L/S books are high-variance.

### Gaps
- No search results were obtained for "relative strength vs BTC" or "beta-neutral alt basket" practitioner results (search budget exhausted before that query ran).
- Monthly 2025 figures for the 1Token Long Short index were not visible in snippets (only mid-2026).
- No evidence on realized funding costs of the short leg for alt L/S in 2024–2025.

## KQ4. Statistical arbitrage / pairs / lead-lag (Binance-leads-other-venues, BTC-leads-alts, cointegration pairs): who reports it working, at what fee tier and latency

### Takeaway
Measured lead-lag horizons are seconds, not minutes: BTC led ADA by 16–118 s (avg ~57 s) at tick level in 2019–2021, cross-exchange BTC lags were up to 15 s in 2018, and Binance led Polymarket by hundreds of milliseconds in 2026; cointegration pairs survive costs on daily data but were loss-making on hourly data in one 2020–2022 study. No practitioner reported a profitable retail-fee implementation.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences

The 10–11 October 2025 cascade is the canonical 2024–2026 failure case: $9.9–19B liquidated (83.9% longs), 70% of it in a 40-minute window, order-book depth at 10 bps down 46%, USDe at $0.65 on Binance only, ADL on Binance and Hyperliquid, Binance ReduceOnly orders failing for 100+ minutes, and compensation >$250–328M; six months later BTC/ETH/XRP/SOL depth was still impaired and alt volumes were down 60%, while funding compression after the event flipped levered carry to negative.

### Cited Findings
**October 10–11, 2025 cascade**
- Size: Amberdata counts $9.89B forcibly closed by 03:00 UTC the next morning; a 40-minute cascade began at 20:50 UTC with 70% of the damage in 40 minutes; forced selling from liquidated longs $8.30B (83.9%) — [Amberdata](https://blog.amberdata.io/how-3.21b-vanished-in-60-seconds-october-2025-crypto-crash-explained-through-7-charts). CoinGecko and CoinShares cite ~$19B over Oct 10–11; another source $19.3B in 24h; near-record OI of $217B beforehand; trigger was the 100% China tariff announcement — [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/october-10-crypto-crash-explained); [CoinShares](https://coinshares.com/insights/knowledge/billions-in-liquidations-what-happened/); [Tianpan memo](https://tianpan.co/investment-memo/2025/10/16/the-1011-crypto-storm). More than $10B of the liquidations were on Hyperliquid — [Finestel](https://finestel.com/blog/hyperliquid-vaults-explained/). Credibility MED-HIGH (data vendors; totals vary by window/provider).
- Depth: order-book depth at 10 bps fell 46% ($48M → $26M) as market makers pulled quotes — [Amberdata depth post](https://blog.amberdata.io/the-liquidity-that-vanished-inside-octobers-40-depth-collapse); one source claims coordinated MM withdrawal cut depth 98% in 40 minutes (contested) — [Proof of Talk](https://proofoftalk.io/blog/crypto-market-fragility-liquidity-leverage/). OKX CEO Star Xu publicly blamed Binance's "irresponsible marketing campaigns" — [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/october-10-crypto-crash-explained). Credibility MED.
- Stablecoin/collateral: USDe fell to ~$0.65 on Binance spot while holding ~$1 on-chain; dislocation confined to Binance's order books; "not a failure of the USDe protocol" per 21Shares — [21Shares](https://www.21shares.com/fr/insights/why-did-ethenas-stablecoin-remain-stable-onchain-but-depegged-on-binance). Cross-venue long/short positions were hurt when ADL hit one leg first, "leaving the hedge gone"; refunds >$250M tied to index/oracle and mint-redeem gaps — [Unchained / The Chopping Block summary](https://unchainedcrypto.com/the-chopping-block-inside-the-19b-perp-crash-adl-explained-binances-usde-staked-token-depeg-and-the-hyperliquid-whale-debate/). A preprint table lists Binance ADL triggered and compensation >$328M by Oct 22 — [ResearchSquare](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-9459584/v1) [UNVERIFIED]. Hyperliquid ADL closed ~$2.1B in 12 minutes — [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/maximizing-the-liquidation-alpha-how-hyperliquid-s-hlp-vault-converts-whale-losses-into-liquidity-provider-yield). Credibility MED.
- Exchange outage: on 11 Oct 2025 Binance's risk-control/order systems rejected exit attempts for 100+ minutes; ReduceOnly orders returned 503 / −1008 / −4118 errors; one market maker lost 591,835 USDT on DOGE and XRP — [Coinedition](https://coinedition.com/binance-system-failure-reduceonly-order-outage/) [single source]. ### Inferences
- Every strategy family in this ledger has an Oct-2025 failure channel: trend (whipsaw/gap), carry (ADL kills one leg; funding flips), MM (stale bids), alt L/S (depth −46%, alt depth still down a third six months later), and operational (ReduceOnly rejected for 100+ minutes). The practical implications for a small perp account: keep leverage low enough to survive a 20–40% alt wick with exits failing for ~2 hours, treat cross-venue hedges as unhedged during cascades, and size carry for a funding regime of 0–5% net rather than the 2024 teens.
- Post-event liquidity did not mean-revert within six months, so cost assumptions calibrated on 2024 depth overstate capacity for alt strategies in 2026.

### Gaps
- No official Binance post-mortem or compensation disclosure was located; compensation figures ($250M vs $328M) are from a podcast summary and a preprint.
- No practitioner quantified realized slippage on alts for a specific strategy during the cascade.
