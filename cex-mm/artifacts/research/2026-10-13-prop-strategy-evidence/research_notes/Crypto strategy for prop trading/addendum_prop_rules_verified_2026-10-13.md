## Breakout (Kraken-owned)
- Current 1-step plans (mid-2026): Classic 10% target / 6% static max DD / 3% daily; Pro 12% / 5% / 3%; Turbo 9% / 3% / 3%. No time limit, no minimum trading days, no consistency rule. Sources: https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/breakout-rules-overview , https://cryptoslate.com/prop-firms/breakout-review/ [secondary]
- Daily loss limit: 3% on every plan, recalculated at **00:30 UTC from the balance at that moment, excluding open positions**, then enforced against **equity including floating P&L** during the day. Max DD is static from the initial balance and is also enforced on equity (touching it once breaches). Sources: https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/breakout-rules-overview , https://www.proptradingvibes.com/blog/breakout-max-drawdown-987bd , https://www.breakoutprop.com/program-rules/ (breach wording) [rules page is primary; the 00:30 UTC detail is secondary]
- Fees inside the evaluation (official help center, https://intercom.help/breakoutprop/en/articles/11647195): **0.04% per buy or sell**, and a **daily swap fee of 0.033% per open position** if held at 00:00 UTC, deducted at 00:25 UTC. These are notional (they reduce evaluation performance; the trader does not pay them in cash during the evaluation). Implication for a daily-rebalanced long-only strategy: it pays 0.033% x gross exposure per day (about 12% a year on notional; at 0.1-0.2x gross that is 1.2-2.4% of equity a year) instead of exchange funding. The local backtest's funding cost (3.4%/yr of equity at 0.2x gross on Binance 2020-26) is of the same order, so the simulator tables are not optimistic on this point [SYNTHETIC comparison].
- Prohibited (program rules page, https://www.breakoutprop.com/program-rules/): any third-party or off-the-shelf strategy, or one marketed to pass evaluations; executing trade ideas belonging to or inspired by a third party (copy trading); latency exploitation; account sharing. Secondary reviews add HFT and cross-account hedging.
- **Bots / API: unresolved.** Third-party guides conflict (one says bots are allowed with no MT4/MT5 so EAs cannot run; another says the terminal has no bot support; a comparison table lists third-party bots as "Restricted"; one review mentions "API abuse detection"). Breakout publishes an article titled "Maximize Your Returns With Algorithmic Prop Trading" (https://www.breakoutprop.com/article/algorithmic-prop-trading/), which suggests algorithmic trading is not banned as such, but the page was not read. Sources: https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/breakout-bot-ea-trading , https://velotrade.com/blog/algo-bot-trading-crypto-prop-firm , https://www.quantvps.com/blog/breakout-crypto-prop-firm-rules . **Action: ask Breakout support in writing whether a self-built, once-a-day systematic strategy executed manually or by the trader's own script is permitted, before paying.** A once-a-day manual rebalance needs no bot and sidesteps the question.
- Weekend/overnight holding: allowed per a secondary review (the help center has an article "Can I hold positions over the weekend in my funded account?", https://intercom.help/breakoutprop/en/collections/13584452-breakout-account, not read). Holding costs the 0.033%/day swap. [UNVERIFIED]
- India: not on Breakout's restricted-country list per two 2026 guides; KYC with Indian ID; payouts in USDC. Sources: https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/breakout-restricted-countries , https://aifo.com/best-prop-firms/india/ [secondary]. Base payout split 80% (not 90%) per https://propfirmpickr.com/prop-firms/breakout [secondary]. Account prices not confirmed by search.

## HyroTrader
- Bot and API access "fully open" (HyroTrader's own blog, https://www.hyrotrader.com/blog/hyrotrader-vs-breakout/); execution on the Bybit API (real exchange) or the CLEO platform on Binance data (https://www.kraken.com/learn/breakout-vs-hyrotrader).
- Mandatory stop-loss within 5 minutes of every trade (first violation soft breach with 1 h to correct, second closes the account); max 3% realized loss per trade; 40% consistency rule in the evaluation only (no single day more than 40% of the net result). Sources: https://www.kraken.com/learn/breakout-vs-hyrotrader , https://www.hyrotrader.com/trading-rules/ , https://proptradingvibes.com/prop-firms/hyrotrader
- Daily loss 4% (1-step; sources conflict for 2-step: 4% or 5%), **trailing by default** with a paid "Swing" upgrade making it static from the day's starting equity; max loss 6% (1-step; 2-step 6% or 10% depending on source). Sources: https://cryptoslate.com/prop-firms/hyrotrader-review/ , https://velotrade.com/prop-firms/hyrotrader [conflicting, UNVERIFIED]. Fee refunded with the first payout.
- Subaccount policy not found.

## India tax on Delta Exchange India perps (unsettled)
- Conservative reading: 30% under s.115BBH (VDA), no loss set-off. Alternative: INR-settled, cash-settled perps are speculative business income under s.43(5)/s.73 (slab rates, losses carried forward 4 years) because no VDA is transferred; no CBDT circular or ruling either way. The Income Tax Act 2025 (effective 1 April 2026) renumbers sections. Sources: https://cleartax.in/s/cryptocurrency-taxation-guide , https://www.caindelhiindia.com/blog/crypto-futures-taxation/ , https://www.caclubindia.com/articles/crypto-futures-tax-in-india-how-speculative-treatment-can-reduce-tax-55965.asp , https://tradesteady.in/crypto-tax-india-2026-spot-futures-options-guide [all secondary; get a written CA opinion].
