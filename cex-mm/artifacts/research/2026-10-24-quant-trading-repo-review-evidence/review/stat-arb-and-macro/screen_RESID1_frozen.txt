Rule (verbatim logic of "Oil Money Trading backtest.py" signal_generation, in ln prices, per alt)
  State: holding in {-1,0,+1}, trained flag, counter.
  Each day t (decision at the 00:00 UTC close):
    if holding != 0: if counter > 10 -> close (holding=0, trained=False, counter=0) and skip to t+1; else counter += 1.
    else:
      if not trained: OLS ln Y = a + b ln BTC on closes [t-50, t-1]; if R^2 > 0.70 -> trained, keep a, b and
        sigma = sd of that window's residual (model frozen until the next trade closes).
      if trained: f_t = a + b ln BTC_t;  ln Y_t > f_t + 2 sigma -> holding = +1;  ln Y_t < f_t - 2 sigma -> holding = -1.
  No price stop (the author's heatmap shows the stop does not matter; README line 111).
Position
  holding = +1: long Y notional N, short b*N BTC.  holding = -1: short Y notional N, long b*N BTC (hedge fixed at entry).
  N = equity / number of alts in the panel (equal notional per alt); Sharpe is reported scale-free.
Fills and costs
  Fill at the 01:00 UTC hourly close after the decision. Every leg fill 5.31 bps taker + 2 bps slippage (7.31 bps/side;
  equal-legged round trip = 4 fills = 29.24 bps of alt notional). Funding on both legs at every stamp. Stress: 10 bps slippage.
Report
  Net Sharpe (365), CAGR, max DD, trades/yr, mean and median net bps per round trip, hit rate, split by long-alt and
  short-alt trades, by calendar year, funding share, correlation of daily returns with the weekly TV 0.25 trend book.
