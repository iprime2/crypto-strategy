# Quant memo: the trend rule is doing exactly what its backtest says, and at retail capital that is pocket money; real money needs about $100k or a different engine
**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Venue:** Delta Exchange India, public data, paper fills (no keys, no orders) · **Books:** daily (TV 0.40, band 0, $1,000, 19 rebalances 8–26 Oct) and weekly (TV 0.25, 10 % band, Monday 00:00 UTC, $1,000, 2 rebalances 19 and 26 Oct) · **Comparator:** the live ADAUSD market maker on Delta mainnet, 20–26 Oct (6,281 venue fills, the wallet ledger) · **Evidence:** `cex-mm/artifacts/research/2026-10-26-trend-strategy-review-evidence/` (journal copies, the three independent scripts sets, the Monte Carlo, the skeptic's recomputation, the comparator reports, and `funding_audit/` for the funding-line cause found later on 26 Oct; see its `README.md`)

## Question
The user asked how the trend strategy is performing, what can be improved, and whether it can earn real money, or whether to move to another strategy.

How it was answered, in three independent passes plus a skeptic:
- **Fidelity.** The two paper journals were re-derived from Delta public candles: the rule's weights recomputed from scratch, a close-to-close backtest (`trend_backtest.py`) run on the same days, every journal identity checked, the tracking error decomposed into rounding, funding and cost.
- **Money.** A Monte Carlo of the owner's dollar outcomes (10,000 and 20,000 paths, Student-t(4) daily returns at the forward Sharpe range of resource/07) at $1k, $5k, $25k and $100k, before tax and under the three tax readings already in the repo, next to an FD, holding BTC, the live market maker and the prop route.
- **Skeptic.** Every headline number recomputed with separate code; one sign error found and corrected (funding was paid, not received); the Sharpe prior challenged.

Nothing was tuned, restarted or changed. The books run on.

## What the code actually does
- **The paper trader is faithful to the rule.** Journal weights equal `target_weights()` recomputed from today's Delta closes to 0.0 on all 19 × 9 daily and 2 × 9 weekly coin-days, both by the repo function and by the skeptic's own implementation from resource/07's formula (`fidelity/fidelity2.py` A, `skeptic/skeptic_signal.py`). The candle history is unrevised since 12 Oct. An independent close-to-close backtest on the same Delta data, 9–25 Oct, gives −1.10 % in whole contracts (−0.96 % unrounded) against the paper book's −1.21 % over the matching 9 Oct 00:05 → 26 Oct 00:05 window; daily-return correlation 0.996. The paper book's extra first period (8 Oct 16:37 → 9 Oct 00:05) made +0.52 % and is outside the backtest.
- **Tracking error is whole-contract rounding and nothing else.** Cumulative paper-minus-model −7.7 bp of equity: rounding −7.3 bp, funding +2.1 bp, cost −0.5 bp. Per period it ranges −14.9 to +25.7 bp. BTC (one contract ≈ $80) and BNB (≈ $74) round to zero on 9 of 19 signal days and to about twice their target on the other 10, so the daily book's gross ran 0.60–1.60x the model's; at $2,500 the ratio is 0.83–0.99, at $5,000 0.95–1.10.
- **The book was long/flat and vol-scaled as designed.** No negative weight on any day; w × vol60 = signal × 0.40 / 9 exactly. Signals over 171 coin-days: flat 21 %, one-third 62 %, full 17 %; the mean signal fell from 1.00 on 7 Oct to 0.15 by 25 Oct, which is why the book was mostly cash while the basket fell. Realised book vol 10.4–11.1 %/yr (convention-dependent); with all nine at full signal the ex-ante vol would be 34 %, not 40 %, because the 60-day pairwise correlation is 0.69.
- **Two housekeeping defects, neither a trading loss:**
  - **The journal under-books funding, and the cause is now known (audit 26 Oct, `funding_audit/`).** Delta's candles endpoint serves a `FUNDING:<sym>` series only from the first 8-hour stamp (00:00, 08:00, 16:00 UTC) at or after the request's `start`, not from the first hour (`endpoint_probe.txt`; a price series starts at the next hour as expected). `trend_paper.py` asked from the last rebalance, 00:05, so it got 17 hourly candles, 08:00 to 00:00, and summed them / 8: the 08:00 and 16:00 exchanges in full and 1/8 of the 00:00 one. That rule reproduces all 18 daily and the weekly journal values from today's candles with zero error at float precision (`exact_match.txt`). So the values the trader read at 00:05 were the published ones: the provisional-candle hypothesis is refuted for all 19 fetches, and the candle history is unrevised as the fidelity pass found. It is refuted forward too: watched minute by minute across the 27 Oct 00:00 exchange, here and on the VM, the new candle appeared within 37 s at its final value and no candle ever changed (`funding_audit/boundary/`).
  - **What the book owed.** Delta's ledger charges, at each 8-hour exchange, position × mark × the rate on the candle stamped at that exchange: 13 of 13 funding entries of the live ADA market maker (22–26 Oct) match it within 0.1 % (one 0.9 %), and the candle before the stamp misses by 2–194 % (`venue_rule_check.txt`). On that rule the daily book owed **$0.4447** over the 18 intervals, not the journal's $0.3533 nor the $0.5632 recompute above, which used the trader's hourly formula over all 24 hours and so is offset by one 8-hour block. Journal minus owed ranges −$0.026 to +$0.048 per interval, beyond $0.01 on 6 of 18; funding is 0.94 %/yr of equity (journal 0.75, hourly recompute 1.20). The model line used the same fetch, so correcting both moves paper-minus-model by only +0.4 bp. Weekly: $0.0899 journaled, $0.0933 owed.
  - **A second, latent defect.** The endpoint answers an empty list for about 5–10 % of these requests (4.6 % and 9.3 % of 108 from the sandbox, 8.2 % of 486 from the VM through the trader's own `fetch_funding`, 26 Oct); the old code booked such a coin's funding as zero. It did not touch the 19 journal rows (the exact match needs no empty answer).
  - **Fixed in `trend_paper.py` on 26 Oct and deployed to the VM on 27 Oct at 08:36 UTC** at the owner's request (only that file and its tests; nothing restarted). The first fixed live row is the daily book's 28 Oct 00:05 rebalance, and the weekly book's is 2 Nov. The fix charges one rate per exchange in (last rebalance, now], asks a coin again when a stamp is missing, and carries a rate Delta has still not published forward to a later rebalance rather than book zero or hold up the rebalance (owner's choice, 27 Oct). It writes `funding_window`, `funding_exchanges` and `funding_rates` (% per 8 h per coin per exchange, null if unpublished) into every journal row, with `funding_pending` (carried) and `funding_late` (settled late). On scratch copies of both books (never the VM's state), the fixed code's first rows match an independent recompute from published candles to $0.00 (`audit_fixed_rows.txt`; `funding_audit.py` audits any journal). On real money the venue settles funding, so none of this was a trading loss.
  - At $1,000 the weekly book is a seven-coin book whenever every signal is at one-third: BTC's target is 0.33 and BNB's 0.34 contracts, both round to zero, and the book expressed 37 % (26 Oct) and 64 % (19 Oct) of its model exposure. resource/07's "from $1,000 rounding is noise" does not hold in this regime; at $2,500 the ratio is 1.13–1.14, at $5,000 0.98–1.12.

## Next experiment (one knob, metric, success, stop)
There is no knob on the trend rule. The one measurable fix was the funding line.
- **Funding audit of `trend_paper.py`: done 26 Oct.** Cause: the candles endpoint drops a FUNDING series' hours before the first 8-hour stamp at or after `start`, plus a latent empty-answer defect; not provisional values. Fix written and tested, with per-row rates and window; success (within $0.01 per interval) met on the fixed code's first rows. The 27 Oct 00:00 boundary check is done: no candle was provisional, the VM's old-code row again equals the old rule to every digit ($0.041 booked against $0.059 owed), and the fixed code on the same starting state books exactly Delta's rule. Its read-only smoke test then found Delta's `FUNDING:<sym>` candle series empty for every coin, resolution and date range (price and mark candles fine; down since some time after 00:20 UTC). At the owner's choice the trader now rebalances on time through such an outage and carries the unpublished funding forward, settling it at the next rebalance after Delta publishes it; redeployed 27 Oct 09:28 UTC (`funding_audit/README.md`, Deploy). Closed on 28 Oct: a re-fetch 24 h later found all 297 snapshot candles unchanged, and the VM's first fixed-code row (00:05 UTC) charged exactly Delta's rule, $0.027989, where the old code would have booked $0.0175 (`funding_audit/README.md`).
- **Fixed re-tests, unchanged:** the annual-review UNI-1 re-run exactly as frozen (`uni1_verify/synthesis.md` §5); TIME-1 only if a bot book ever replaces hand execution; resource/07 kill #6.
- **Process sheet instead of an equity chart**, monthly: executed-on-time count; cost per $ traded (7.31 model, 14.62 gate) and the cost rate per year of equity; paper-minus-model drift; funding paid as %/yr of equity (failure signature > 2 %); gross vs cap; once live, venue position = order list, leverage ≤ 5x, cross margin. Any missed slot twice in a row, a position not on the list, median fill worse than 14.62 bps over ten orders, or funding above 2 %/yr: stop and fix the process.

## Limits
- 19 daily and 2 weekly rebalances are a plumbing and cost check; every percentile and years-to-reject figure is a normal or i.i.d. approximation; the Monte Carlo has no volatility clustering, so its drawdown and pause frequencies are lower bounds and its tails are kind (2025-10-10-type days are fatter than t(4)).
- The 12 %-vol weekly book and 19 %-vol daily book are the backtest's full-sample figures; the daily book has realised 10.4–11.1 % in 19 days because signals were weak. Rounding, the 30 %-on-venue margin dynamics and BTC/BNB rounding to zero are not in the Monte Carlo, so its $-per-month at $1k–$2.5k is too high by up to half in weak-signal regimes.
- The tax model is a linear fit of six backtest years; the 30 % slab and no cess are assumptions about the owner's bracket; the FD rate, Delta's INR settlement, the CA fee and every prop-firm fact are [UNVERIFIED]. Venue risk (30 % of trend capital and 100 % of the market maker's $325 on Delta India) is unpriced in every comparator.
- The market-maker comparator rests on 6.27 days of ledger in one regime, a revocable 1.25 bps maker fee, +496 ADA of open inventory at the end, and a capacity model whose sweep-size tail comes from 50 public prints; its earlier real-money run (13 Oct, old fair) lost −3.6 bps per fill.
- The live marks sent to Telegram at 13:46 UTC ($992.66 / $996.83) are not reproducible from the copied data; the rebalance marks ($992.94 / $997.00) are.
