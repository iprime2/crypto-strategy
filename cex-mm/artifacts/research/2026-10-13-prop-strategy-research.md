# Quant memo: which strategy to build for own capital and prop-firm accounts
**Date:** 2026-10-13 · **Venue:** Delta India mainnet public data and the Binance USDT-M perp archive as a proxy (no orders placed) · **Symbols:** BTC, ETH, XRP, BNB, ADA, LTC, LINK, DOGE, BCH perps · **Data:** daily and hourly closes plus funding 2020-05..2026-09 (Binance), 2024-08..2026-10 (Delta) · **Full report:** `resource/07-trend-rule-and-prop-trading-research.md` · **Evidence:** `cex-mm/artifacts/research/2026-10-13-prop-strategy-evidence/` (seven research notes, three designs, the pre-registered backtest `backtest_results.md`, run outputs and scripts) · **Tools added:** `scripts/trend_hourly.py` (hourly mark-to-market of the trend rule), `scripts/prop_sim.py` (prop-challenge Monte Carlo), both with `--selftest`

## Question
The user asked for a robust, systematic crypto strategy to make money with their own capital and through crypto prop-firm funded accounts, with the logic explained. Research covered prop-firm rules, the academic and practitioner evidence on crypto return predictability, funding carry, alternative signals, risk and execution on Delta India, Indian tax, and the challenge mathematics; three designs were judged, the winner was backtested with a pre-registered plan and then attacked by three adversarial reviewers.

## Outcome
- **Strategy:** the repo's existing long/flat time-series-momentum rule (`scripts/trend_backtest.py::target_weights`: mean of sign(20/60/120-day return) floored at zero, 60-day realized-vol sizing, 9 fixed Delta perps), run at **target vol 0.25** *(start)* instead of 0.40, decided once a week at the Monday 00:00 UTC candle, executed by hand with market orders, 10% no-trade band, whole contracts, **cross margin, leverage ≤ 5x, at most 30% of capital on the venue**. No bot, no intraday rules, no second engine.
- **Everything else was rejected on its own after-cost evidence:** market making lost −3.6 bps net per fill live (`2026-10-13-live-run-pickoff.md`), daily option selling has no out-of-sample edge (`options-research/`), funding carry's Sharpe went from 6.45 (2020–25) to negative in 2025 and Delta's round trip plus 1% TDS kills it, and ML, on-chain, sentiment, intraday seasonality, cross-sectional alts, lead-lag and event signals all fail the ~10 bps cost bar [UNVERIFIED snippets, see the report].
- **Prop firms:** the only leg that can pay four figures. Breakout Classic 1-step (10% target, 3% daily loss on the 00:30 UTC balance, 6% static max DD, no time limit; fees $275 at $25k, $999 at $100k; 80% split) is the best fit, with the same rule run **daily at half size (k = 0.5, about 10% vol) and resting per-position stops** as the daily-limit protection. **Deferred** until a written CA opinion, 26 executed live weeks, and written answers from the firm on its bot/script policy, coin list, reset mechanics, inactivity rule and India eligibility, none of which could be read on the firm's own pages from this sandbox.

## What the code actually does (vs the design)
- Signal and sizing exist in `scripts/trend_backtest.py` and `scripts/trend_paper.py` (daily, TV 0.40). Built 2026-10-14: `trend_paper.py --weekly --target-vol 0.25 --band 0.10 --state-dir artifacts/trend/paper-weekly` runs the design's configuration as a second book (unit `cex-mm-trend-paper-weekly.timer`, dashboard `/trend?book=weekly`), and `--orders-only` prints the Monday order list from the same sizing code for hand execution. A state directory refuses a run with different settings. Tests: `python3 -m unittest test_trend_paper` in `scripts/`.
- `mm-bot`, `mm-oems`, the DISM profile and the Binance anchor are not used by this strategy. The bot's post-only invariant is untouched.

## Next experiment (one knob, metric, success, stop)
Week 0: commission the CA opinion (gate 0) and reproduce T5 on live Delta candles. Weeks 1–4: paper-trade the weekly TV 0.25 configuration next to the daily reference; one 1-contract round trip per coin to read real fees and slippage. **Gate A:** every rebalance executed, measured cost ≤ 2 × 7.31 bps per side, hand-computed and scripted order lists identical on four Mondays, CA opinion favourable. Week 5: live at $1,000 *(start)*, 30% on venue. **Gate B (week 8):** four of four Mondays executed, no kill fired. Then run to week 26 before any prop fee. **Stop:** two missed Mondays, a reconciliation mismatch, median fill worse than 2 × 7.31 bps over ten orders, a withdrawal over 7 business days, or a CA opinion that VDA-no-set-off applies. Pause (not kill): equity 20% below peak at a Monday check.

## Limits
All web facts are search snippets (the sandbox proxy blocked every external page); the prop section is conditional on reading the firm's rules. The backtest is Binance data as a Delta proxy (prices within 0–2.5 bps median, correlation 0.998–1.000; Delta funding differs). The simulators are hourly, so every "0% daily breach" is a lower bound. The post-2021 edge is a point estimate whose interval includes zero; 26 live weeks test adherence, not edge.
